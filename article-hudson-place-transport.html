Why Location Matters for Investors

Properties within a 10-minute commute to the CBD command a premium in both rental and resale markets. Hudson Place's position puts it squarely in that category — you're 7 minutes on the NEL from Raffles Place, which is competitive even with CCR condos at half the price per square foot.

The freehold tenure adds another layer of value. In a market where 99-year leaseholds dominate new launches, freehold condos near the city centre maintain their value better over time and appeal to a wider buyer pool.

Who Should Consider Hudson Place?

  • CBD professionals — 12-min door-to-door commute is hard to beat at this price point
  • Investors targeting young renters — proximity to Little India, Bugis, and Orchard attracts young professionals
  • Freehold buyers — genuine freehold in D8 is increasingly rare
  • Upgraders staying central — HDB upgraders from Kallang/Whampoa who don't want to leave the area

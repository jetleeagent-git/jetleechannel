Nestled at 3 & 5 Marina Gardens Lane in District 1, One Marina Gardens sits within the vibrant Marina South waterfront precinct, offering direct covered linkway access to the future Marina South MRT on the Thomson-East Coast Line.
Comprising 937 beautifully designed units across 8 bedroom types, One Marina Gardens ranges from a cosy 1 Bedroom at 420 sqft to a spacious 4 Bedroom Premium at 1,647 sqft, rising across 2 towers of 30 and 44 storeys.

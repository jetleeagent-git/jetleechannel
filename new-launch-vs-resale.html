New Launch vs Resale Condo — Which is Better in 2026?

By Jet Lee · 12 June 2026 · 5 min read

One of the most common questions I get from buyers: "Should I buy a new launch or a resale condo?"

There is no one-size-fits-all answer. Both options have distinct advantages depending on your financial situation, timeline, and goals. In this article, I break down the key differences so you can decide which path suits you best.

New Launch — Pros and Cons

✅ Advantages

  • Progressive payment — You only pay as construction progresses. Much more manageable than a lump sum
  • Low initial outlay — Typically 5% booking fee, then payments spread over 3-4 years
  • Brand new — Latest designs, smart home features, energy-efficient fittings
  • Defect liability — Developers fix any defects within the first 12 months
  • Capital appreciation — Prices often rise during construction, giving you paper gains before TOP
  • No immediate Reno — New units are move-in ready with modern finishing

❌ Disadvantages

  • Waiting time — 3-5 years before you can move in or collect rent
  • Uncertainty — Final quality, surrounding developments, and market conditions at TOP are unknown
  • Higher psf — Developers price in a premium for the new product
  • Limited choice — For balance units, only leftovers remain

Resale — Pros and Cons

✅ Advantages

  • Move in immediately — No waiting for TOP. Ideal if you need a home now
  • Immediate rental income — Buy and rent out right away
  • Negotiable pricing — You can negotiate with the seller, sometimes below valuation
  • See what you get — No guesswork. You see the actual unit, the view, the finishes
  • Established estate — Mature landscaping, known facility quality, and community feel

❌ Disadvantages

  • Lump sum payment — Need 25% down payment within 8-10 weeks (or 24% for HDB)
  • High cash requirement — Especially if the valuation is below the purchase price (cash over valuation)
  • May need renovation — Older units may require costly upgrades
  • Shorter lease — Especially for older condos, the lease decay affects resale value over time

Which One Should You Choose?

Choose New Launch if:

  • You do not need a home immediately and can wait 3-5 years
  • You prefer a lower initial cash outlay and progressive payments
  • You want the latest design, smart features, and a full defect period
  • You are an investor looking for potential capital appreciation before TOP
  • You are an HDB upgrader who wants to sell your HDB later, closer to TOP date

Choose Resale if:

  • You need to move in or start collecting rent immediately
  • You have sufficient cash for a lump sum down payment
  • You prefer seeing the actual unit before committing
  • You want to negotiate the price and potentially find a bargain
  • You want to avoid construction uncertainty

A Third Option: Balance Units

There is actually a middle ground — balance units. These are new launch units that were not sold during launch but are still under construction. They give you the benefits of a new launch (progressive payment, brand new, defect period) with the advantage of being able to negotiate with the developer.

For example, ELTA at Clementi still has balance units available. You get brand new specs, smart home features, progressive payment, and the developer may offer incentives to close the deal. It is the best of both worlds if you find the right unit.

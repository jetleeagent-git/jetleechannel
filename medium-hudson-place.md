## The One-North Transformation: A 20-Year Play Coming to Fruition

One-north was conceived as Singapore's Silicon Valley — a purpose-built innovation district spanning 200 hectares across Mediapolis, Fusionopolis, Biopolis, and the future one-north Park. For years, the story was about potential. Now, it's about delivery.

**What's changed in 2025-2026:**

- **Budget 2026 doubled down on AI** — Singapore committed billions to AI research, talent development, and computing infrastructure. One-north is ground zero for this push.
- **Mediapolis is now fully operational** — Netflix Asia HQ, Disney's APAC content hub, Ubisoft, and over 100 media and tech companies are already there. This isn't a future plan; it's happening now.
- **Grab HQ and Mediacorp** anchor the surrounding employment corridor.
- **NUS and INSEAD** provide the talent pipeline.

Hudson Place sits at **Blk 18 & 20 Media Circle** — right in the heart of Mediapolis. For professionals working in one-north, this is a genuine live-work-play proposition. For investors, it's a corridor with structural tenant demand.

## Why District 5 Is Outperforming

Hudson Place is in District 5, a corridor that's quietly outperforming many "hotter" districts in 2025-2026. Why?

1. **One-north employment density** — Over 60,000 professionals working in R&D, media, and tech within a 2km radius
2. **Limited new supply** — Few GLS sites remaining in the one-north precinct
3. **Queenstown Node transformation** — The Greater Southern Waterfront and Queenstown redevelopment add long-term upside
4. **International school catchment** — Tanglin Trust, ACS (Independent), UWC Dover within easy reach

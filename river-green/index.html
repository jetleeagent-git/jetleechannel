Project Overview

Exclusive Living on the River Valley

River Green is an exclusive new launch in District 09 by WINCHAMP INVESTMENT PTE LTD, perfectly located in the heart of the River Valley cluster. This prestigious 36-storey tower comprises 524 units rising above a beautifully landscaped podium with facilities befitting a D9 address.

With a site-sensitive design approach, River Green places residents a prime location next to Great World City MRT — an E-deck berm raised 5m from street level, with units raised another 16m (equivalent to 5 storeys) for elevated privacy and views. The roof feature combined with façade lighting enhances the city skyline.

Unit plans are compact, functional and efficient — all units are non-PPVC with flexible internal layouts, universal design and sustainability efforts (Greenmark Platinum, SLE 5-Badge).

Location

Great World MRT Next Door

River Green is right next to Great World MRT (17m), with Havelock MRT 902m, Somerset 1.27km, Orchard 1.43km and Fort Canning 1.54km. One stop to Orchard Road, minutes to the CBD. The River Valley Green area offers riverside promenades, supermarkets and F&B at Great World City, with childcare and schools nearby.

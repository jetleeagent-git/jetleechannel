P1 Registration Phase 2C: Nearly Half of Primary Schools Oversubscribed — What It Means for Buyers

Published 6 August 2026 · Schools & Market Update

If you're a parent — or planning to become one near a popular primary school — this year's Primary 1 registration numbers deserve your attention.

According to MOE data released on Wednesday (Aug 5), 79 of 179 primary schools accepting students in Phase 2C are oversubscribed. Of those with more applicants than vacancies, 73 will go to the ballot.

Phase 2C is the most competitive phase — open to all children, with 40 places reserved in each primary school. For property buyers, it's a direct signal about which school zones carry the strongest demand.

The headline numbers: 179 primary schools accepted students in Phase 2C · 79 oversubscribed (nearly half) · 73 going to ballot. Most oversubscribed: Princess Elizabeth Primary (Bukit Batok) — 159 applicants for 40 places. Northland Primary (Yishun) — 141 applicants for 42 places. Nan Hua, Chongfu and Rosyth completed the top five.

How Balloting Works — and Why 1km Matters

Balloting happens when applicants exceed vacancies. Priority admission order:

  1. Singaporeans living within 1km of the school
  2. Singaporeans living between 1km and 2km
  3. Singaporeans living further than 2km
  4. Permanent residents in the same distance groups

The 1km advantage is decisive — that's why "within 1km of a popular school" has historically commanded a premium in Singapore property. This year's data confirms the pattern hasn't changed.

Bigger Picture: Fewer Spots Overall

There's another layer: MOE earlier cut the Primary 1 intake for most schools this year in view of significantly falling student cohort sizes from 2027.

  • Compared to 2025, there are 1,460 fewer spots overall in 2026
  • Of 179 primary schools, 61 cut their intake while 12 increased spaces

Smaller cohorts mean fewer places — even as demand for popular schools stays intense. The net effect: oversubscription pressure concentrates in the schools parents most want.

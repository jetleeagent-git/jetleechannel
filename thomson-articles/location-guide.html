Thomson Reserve Location Guide — MRT, Schools, Parks & Amenities

Published June 2026 · District 20, Upper Thomson

Thomson Reserve (汤申保留地) occupies one of the most coveted addresses in Singapore's District 20 — Bright Hill Drive, Upper Thomson. Beyond the 1,268-unit mega development itself, the location is what truly sets this project apart. Nestled beside MacRitchie Reservoir and serviced by the brand-new Thomson-East Coast MRT Line, residents enjoy a rare combination of nature and urban convenience.

MRT Connectivity — Thomson-East Coast Line (TEL)

The TEL is Singapore's newest MRT line, and Thomson Reserve is perfectly positioned along it:

  • Upper Thomson MRT (TE8) — Immediately adjacent to the site. Exit the condo and you're steps from the station entrance. Connects directly to Orchard (via Stevens), Marina Bay, and Gardens by the Bay.
  • Bright Hill MRT (TE7) — Approximately 5 minutes' walk. An interchange with the Cross Island Line (CRL) when it opens, making it a key transport hub.
  • Mayflower MRT (TE6) — Also about 5 minutes away, serving the Sin Ming/Mayflower neighbourhood.

From Upper Thomson MRT, you can reach Orchard in about 15 minutes, Marina Bay in 25 minutes, and Woodlands North in 20 minutes. The TEL is fully underground, air-conditioned, and runs at high frequency during peak hours.

Key travel times from Upper Thomson MRT:
Orchard — 15 min · Marina Bay — 25 min · Bishan (NSL interchange) — 2 stops · Caldecott (CCL interchange) — 3 stops · Woodlands — 20 min

Top Schools in the Vicinity

Within 1km — Ai Tong School

Ai Tong School is one of the most established SAP schools in Singapore, with a rich history dating back to 1912. It is a popular choice among parents for its strong academic track record and emphasis on bilingual education. Being within 1km of Thomson Reserve means priority in Phase 2C balloting — a significant advantage for families with young children.

Within 1–2km

  • CHIJ St Nicholas Girls' School — Renowned all-girls' mission school, primary and secondary sections
  • Catholic High School (Primary) — Leading SAP boys' school with strong bilingual programme

Other Nearby Schools

  • Anglo-Chinese School (Primary) — Driving distance
  • Nanyang Primary School — One of the top primary schools, within reasonable distance
  • Eunoia Junior College — Newest JC, located at Bishan
  • Nanyang Junior College — At Bishan, approximately 10 minutes' drive
  • Singapore American School (Woodlands) — Reachable via TEL

Nature & Parks — A Green Corridor

This is arguably Thomson Reserve's strongest selling point. The site sits at the edge of Singapore's largest green corridor:

  • MacRitchie Reservoir Park — Right next door. Home to the famous TreeTop Walk, HSBC Treetop Walk, and over 11km of hiking trails. Kayaking and fishing available.
  • Windsor Nature Park — Adjacent to MacRitchie. A quieter, beautifully maintained park with boardwalk trails through secondary forest. About 5 minutes' walk from Thomson Reserve.
  • Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park — A 10-minute drive or quick bus ride. Singapore's largest urban park with river restoration, dog run, and picnic lawns.
  • Lower Peirce Reservoir — A short drive north. Secluded and serene, ideal for cycling and jogging.
Did you know? Over 80% of the Thomson Reserve site is dedicated to lush landscaping, with the development designed as a "sanctuary within the city" — a natural extension of the adjacent reservoir park.

Shopping & Dining

  • Thomson Plaza — Directly opposite the site across Upper Thomson Road. A mid-sized mall with FairPrice Finest, food court, Daiso, shops, and a cinema.
  • Upper Thomson Road Food Street — About 5 minutes' walk. This stretch is famous for its long-running eateries: Sin Hoe Kee Seafood (螃蟹米粉), Casuarina Curry, White Rose Cafe (nasi lemak), Mookata BBQ, and countless cafes.
  • Junction 8 (Bishan) — Approximately 10 minutes via MRT or bus. Full shopping mall with major retail, food court, cineplex.
  • Thomson V One — A smaller mall nearby with popular dim sum and eateries.

Bright Hill Drive — Site Context

The former Thomson View Condominium (255 units) stood on this 504,314 sqft site for decades. Acquired via en bloc for S$810 million ($1,178 psf ppr), it was one of the largest collective sales in Singapore. The site enjoys a plot ratio of 2.1 and faces MacRitchie's greenery on two sides, giving many units unobstructed panoramic views from Level 15 and above.

FAQ

How far is Thomson Reserve from Upper Thomson MRT?

The site sits immediately beside Upper Thomson MRT station (TE8). The station entrance is within walking distance from the condo's main gate.

Is Ai Tong School within 1km of Thomson Reserve?

Yes. Ai Tong School is within 1km of Thomson Reserve, which gives priority in Phase 2C of the Primary One Registration Exercise. Catholic High School and CHIJ St Nicholas Girls' School are within 1–2km.

What amenities are nearby for daily shopping?

Thomson Plaza is directly opposite the site. Upper Thomson Road itself has numerous eateries and shops. Junction 8 at Bishan is just 10 minutes away by MRT.

What type of nature activities are available nearby?

MacRitchie Reservoir Park and Windsor Nature Park are adjacent to the development. Hiking, jogging, kayaking, fishing, and the TreeTop Walk are all accessible directly from the condo.

When is the expected TOP for Thomson Reserve?

The estimated TOP is TBC (to be confirmed). The targeted preview is October 2026, subject to authorities' approval.

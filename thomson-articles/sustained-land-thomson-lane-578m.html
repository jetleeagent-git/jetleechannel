Sustained Land Buys Thomson Lane Plot for S$578M — 770+ Unit Condo Planned on Prime D11 Site

Published 19 August 2026 · Source: The Business Times (18 August 2026)

A joint venture led by developer Sustained Land is buying a sprawling site at 8 Thomson Lane for S$578 million from an entity linked to OCBC's Lee family, with plans to build a large residential project on the prime plot.

Deal snapshot: 8 Thomson Lane (formerly 418 Thomson Road) · S$578 million · ~203,000 sq ft site · zoned for residential redevelopment at gross plot ratio up to 3.5 · potentially 770+ residential units · 105-year leasehold tenure carved out of the freehold title · District 11, between Novena and Toa Payoh.

Who Is Sustained Land?

Founded and owned by low-profile tycoon Douglas Ong, Sustained Land is a Singapore-based property developer specialising in residential developments. To date, it has 17 assets in its portfolio, including:

  • One Meyer — Marine Parade
  • Sky Everton — near Tanjong Pagar
  • The Sen — a 347-unit condominium in Upper Bukit Timah launched in late 2025

Sustained Land is undertaking the Thomson Lane acquisition with construction group Kay Lim Realty, and holds the majority stake in the joint venture.

A Site With a Rich History

The site at 8 Thomson Lane has a storied past, having served multiple roles over the decades:

  • 1940s: A historic country bungalow once stood here, overlooking the Kallang River. The grand colonial-style building served as an early base for Redemptorist priests.
  • 1950s–1960s: Became the Chequers Hotel, enjoying its heyday before closing in April 1985.
  • ~2000s: Transformed into Europa Country Club Resort, which closed in March 2006.
  • ~2007: The Celebrities Resort Club was planned (started by former MediaCorp actor Lin Yisheng and partners) but never took off.
  • 2014–2023: EtonHouse International School operated a campus here for about nine years until its closure in 2023.
  • 2024: Chequers Properties obtained permission to demolish the existing buildings and swimming pool.
  • 2025: Plans were submitted for a commercial development (four-storey office podium + 28-storey office tower/medical suites) — but URA rejected the proposal, as the commercial use at GPR 3.0 was not in line with the predominantly residential planning intention for the area.

An Up-and-Coming Precinct

Located in prime District 11, the site is tucked between Novena and Toa Payoh. It is close to:

  • Singapore Polo Club
  • Retail amenities at United Square
  • The Novena area medical cluster
  • Schools including St Joseph's Institution International and CHIJ Secondary (Toa Payoh)

The site is also within walking distance of the future Mount Pleasant MRT station and Toa Payoh MRT station.

Nearby, the upcoming Mount Pleasant housing estate is being developed on the former site of the Old Police Academy. The 33-hectare estate in the Core Central Region is expected to yield about 6,000 public housing units across four Build-To-Order projects — a major regeneration that will transform the wider area.

What This Means for Thomson Reserve & Upper Thomson

For buyers considering Thomson Reserve in Upper Thomson (District 20), this deal is a useful benchmark for where the broader Thomson corridor is heading:

  • Confidence in the Thomson belt: A S$578 million land purchase near Novena signals continued developer conviction in the Thomson/Novena corridor, which shares the same rail spine (Thomson-East Coast Line) as Upper Thomson.
  • District 11 regeneration: With the Mount Pleasant estate adding ~6,000 homes and the Thomson Lane site adding 770+ units, the area between Novena and Toa Payoh is being densified and rejuvenated — boosting the entire northern corridor's appeal.
  • Land cost context: At ~S$1,293 psf ppr (including betterment charge), this D11 site costs more per plot ratio than Thomson Reserve's own Thomson View land (~S$1,178 psf ppr) — evidence that prime freehold-converted sites command a premium, while Upper Thomson offers comparatively compelling value.
  • Rental & resale ecosystem: More new supply in the corridor creates a larger, more liquid market — with distinct sub-markets from Novena to Bright Hill rather than a single competing mass.
The takeaway for buyers: The S$578 million Thomson Lane deal reinforces the strength of the entire Thomson-to-Novena corridor. For buyers weighing District 20's Thomson Reserve against pricier D11 land, the comparison favours Upper Thomson's value proposition — the same connectivity story at a more accessible price point.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much did Sustained Land pay for the Thomson Lane site?

S$578 million for the site at 8 Thomson Lane, with a further ~S$436 million estimated betterment charge for the residential redevelopment — bringing the total land cost to more than S$1 billion, or close to S$1,293 psf ppr.

How many units will the Thomson Lane condo have?

With the higher plot ratio (up to 3.5) and a 10% bonus GFA, the site could yield about 781,800 sq ft of GFA and potentially more than 770 residential units, according to industry players.

What is the tenure of the Thomson Lane site?

The S$578 million site was sold with a 105-year leasehold tenure carved out of the freehold title. Market watchers note this longer tenure gives the developer more time to complete the project while allowing eventual residential units to be sold with around 99 years or more remaining on their leases.

Is this near Thomson Reserve?

Thomson Reserve is in Upper Thomson (District 20), further north along the Thomson-East Coast Line. The Thomson Lane site is in District 11, between Novena and Toa Payoh — roughly 10–15 minutes away by car, sharing the same rail corridor.

When will the Thomson Lane condo launch?

A launch timeline has not been announced. The developer still needs to complete the redevelopment approval process. Buyers interested in the wider Thomson corridor can meanwhile register interest for Thomson Reserve, which targets preview in October 2026.

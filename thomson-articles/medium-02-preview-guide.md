*Updated June 2026 · Expected Preview: October 2026*

Thomson Reserve (汤申保留地) is one of the most anticipated new launch condos in Singapore for 2026. With an expected preview in October 2026, this mega development by UOL Group, CapitaLand Development, and Singapore Land Group has already generated significant interest.

This guide covers everything you need to know about the launch — from the timeline and how to register, to what to expect at the showflat and the booking process.

**Launch Timeline (Current Status)**

As of June 2026, the following timeline applies. All dates are subject to authorities' approval:

- **Targeted Preview: October 2026** — Invitation-only preview for registered VVIPs. E-brochure and indicative price guide typically released around this time.
- **Public Launch: After preview phase** — Units opened to the general public.
- **Balloting: As needed** — If demand exceeds supply, a balloting exercise will be conducted by the developer.
- **Estimated TOP: TBC** — Major projects of this scale typically take 3–5 years from preview.

> **Important:** The preview date of October 2026 is a *targeted* timeline. Register your interest early to receive updates directly.

**How to Register for the VVIP Preview**

1. **Register your interest** on the official Thomson Reserve website via the contact form.
2. **Meet the developer's sales team** to indicate your unit preferences and budget.
3. **Submit your cheque** (if required) during the preview booking phase.
4. **Attend the showflat** on your allocated date/time slot.

**What to Expect at the Showflat**
- **Show units** — Fully furnished examples of selected unit types (1+S, 2BR, 3BR, 4BR)
- **Site model** — Scale model showing master plan, block positions, and landscaping
- **Material board** — Samples of finishes, flooring, countertops, and fittings
- **Price list** — Unit-by-unit pricing (released during preview)
- **QQ sheets** — Charts showing available units across price points

**Types of Buyers Attending**
- **Owner-occupiers** — Families drawn by Ai Tong School proximity and nature setting
- **Upgraders** — HDB flat owners in Bishan, Ang Mo Kio, Upper Thomson
- **Investors** — Attracted by three blue-chip developers and rental demand
- **Foreign buyers** — Eligible buyers attracted by the unique reservoir-side location

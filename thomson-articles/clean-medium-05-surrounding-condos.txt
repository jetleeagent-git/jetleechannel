*Published June 2026*

Thomson Reserve sits in the heart of Upper Thomson, surrounded by a mix of established condominiums and new launches. Whether you're considering Thomson Reserve as your next home or exploring the area for investment, knowing the neighbourhood's existing and upcoming developments gives you a clearer picture of the location's value.

**Bright Hill Drive — Direct Neighbours**

**Thomson View Condominium (Former)**
*Status: En bloc sold 2024, demolished — site of Thomson Reserve*
The 255-unit Thomson View stood on this 504,314 sqft site for decades before the S$810 million collective sale. The site has been cleared for Thomson Reserve's development of ~1,268 units.

**Spring Grove (Next Door)**
*Status: Completed mixed landed cluster*
A cluster of semi-detached and terrace houses directly adjacent to the Thomson Reserve land parcel, enjoying the same proximity to MacRitchie Reservoir and Upper Thomson MRT.

**New Launches Nearby**

**Lentor Hills Estate (Lentor MRT Area)**
*~5–8 min drive*
Recent launches include Lentor Hills Residences, The Arcady @ Lentor, Lentor Modern (sold out), and Hillock Green. While Lentor offers new infrastructure, Upper Thomson (where Thomson Reserve sits) has a more established neighbourhood with mature trees, MacRitchie greenery, and Thomson Plaza directly opposite.

**The Arcady @ Lentor**
*Developer: CDL / Mitsubishi / Frasers · ~7 min drive*
Thomson Reserve's edge lies in its direct adjacency to MacRitchie Reservoir, Upper Thomson's dining scene, and being within 1km of Ai Tong School.

**Established Condos in Upper Thomson**

**Thomson Grand**
*Sin Ming Walk (~5 min drive)*
Completed 99-year leasehold condo by UOL Group (same developer as Thomson Reserve). Its resale performance gives a useful reference for Thomson Reserve's future value.

**Thomson Grove**
*Thomson Ridge (~8 min drive)*
Older freehold condo with quiet residential setting and spacious layouts. Commands a tenure premium but lacks MRT proximity and modern facilities.

**Shaughnessy**
*Ang Mo Kio Ave 2 (~3 min drive)*
Exclusive low-density development with only 48 units near Singapore Island Country Club.

**8 @ Hutchison**
*Upper Thomson Road (~3 min drive)*
Popular among families for its proximity to Catholic High School and CHIJ St Nicholas Girls'.

**Landed Enclaves**
- Bright Hill Drive — Directly surrounding Thomson Reserve
- Thomson Ridge — Prime GCB area
- Poh Huat Road / Poh Huat Terrace — Established landed estate

**FAQ**

*Is Thomson Reserve better located than nearby Lentor Hills projects?*
Thomson Reserve offers a more established neighbourhood with direct MacRitchie Reservoir access, while Lentor Hills is a newly planned estate. Thomson Reserve is closer to Thomson Plaza and within 1km of Ai Tong School.

*What resale condos near Thomson Reserve should I compare prices with?*
Thomson Grand, 8 @ Hutchison, and The Arcady @ Lentor are the most comparable. None share Thomson Reserve's unique reservoir-side location.

*Are there any other new launches planned in Upper Thomson?*
Thomson Reserve is the only new mega launch in the immediate Upper Thomson area.

*What is the nearest shopping mall?*
Thomson Plaza is directly opposite. Junction 8 at Bishan is about 10 minutes by MRT.

*How does Thomson Reserve compare to older freehold condos?*
Freehold condos command a tenure premium, but Thomson Reserve offers modern facilities, direct MRT access, and quality from three blue-chip developers.

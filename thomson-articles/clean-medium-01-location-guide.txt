*Published June 2026 · District 20, Upper Thomson*

Thomson Reserve (汤申保留地) occupies one of the most coveted addresses in Singapore's District 20 — Bright Hill Drive, Upper Thomson. Beyond the 1,268-unit mega development itself, the location is what truly sets this project apart. Nestled beside MacRitchie Reservoir and serviced by the brand-new Thomson-East Coast MRT Line, residents enjoy a rare combination of nature and urban convenience.

**MRT Connectivity — Thomson-East Coast Line (TEL)**

The TEL is Singapore's newest MRT line, and Thomson Reserve is perfectly positioned along it:

- **Upper Thomson MRT (TE8)** — Immediately adjacent to the site. Exit the condo and you're steps from the station entrance. Connects directly to Orchard (via Stevens), Marina Bay, and Gardens by the Bay.
- **Bright Hill MRT (TE7)** — Approximately 5 minutes' walk. An interchange with the Cross Island Line (CRL) when it opens, making it a key transport hub.
- **Mayflower MRT (TE6)** — Also about 5 minutes away, serving the Sin Ming/Mayflower neighbourhood.

From Upper Thomson MRT, you can reach Orchard in about 15 minutes, Marina Bay in 25 minutes, and Woodlands North in 20 minutes.

> **Key travel times:** Orchard — 15 min · Marina Bay — 25 min · Bishan (NSL interchange) — 2 stops · Caldecott (CCL interchange) — 3 stops · Woodlands — 20 min

**Top Schools in the Vicinity**

*Within 1km — Ai Tong School*

Ai Tong School is one of the most established SAP schools in Singapore, with a rich history dating back to 1912. Being within 1km of Thomson Reserve means priority in Phase 2C balloting — a significant advantage for families.

*Within 1–2km*
- CHIJ St Nicholas Girls' School — Renowned all-girls' mission school
- Catholic High School (Primary) — Leading SAP boys' school

*Other Nearby Schools*
- Anglo-Chinese School (Primary), Nanyang Primary School, Eunoia Junior College, Nanyang Junior College, Singapore American School

**Nature & Parks — A Green Corridor**

This is arguably Thomson Reserve's strongest selling point:
- **MacRitchie Reservoir Park** — Right next door. Home to the TreeTop Walk and over 11km of hiking trails.
- **Windsor Nature Park** — Adjacent to MacRitchie. About 5 minutes' walk.
- **Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park** — A 10-minute drive.
- **Lower Peirce Reservoir** — Short drive north.

> **Did you know?** Over 80% of the Thomson Reserve site is dedicated to lush landscaping — a true sanctuary within the city.

**Shopping & Dining**
- **Thomson Plaza** — Directly opposite the site across Upper Thomson Road.
- **Upper Thomson Road Food Street** — About 5 minutes' walk. Famous for Sin Hoe Kee Seafood, Casuarina Curry, White Rose Cafe, and countless cafes.
- **Junction 8 (Bishan)** — Approximately 10 minutes via MRT.

**Bright Hill Drive — Site Context**

The former Thomson View Condominium (255 units) stood on this 504,314 sqft site for decades. Acquired via en bloc for S$810 million ($1,178 psf ppr). The site faces MacRitchie's greenery on two sides, giving many units unobstructed panoramic views from Level 15 and above.

**FAQ**

*How far is Thomson Reserve from Upper Thomson MRT?*
The site sits immediately beside Upper Thomson MRT station (TE8). The station entrance is within walking distance.

*Is Ai Tong School within 1km of Thomson Reserve?*
Yes. It's within 1km, giving priority in Phase 2C of the Primary One Registration Exercise.

*What amenities are nearby for daily shopping?*
Thomson Plaza is directly opposite. Upper Thomson Road has numerous eateries and shops.

*What type of nature activities are available nearby?*
MacRitchie Reservoir Park and Windsor Nature Park are adjacent. Hiking, jogging, kayaking, fishing, and the TreeTop Walk are all accessible from the condo.

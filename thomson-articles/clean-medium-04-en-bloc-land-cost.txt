*Published June 2026*

Thomson Reserve occupies the site of the former Thomson View Condominium, which was sold via en bloc in one of Singapore's largest collective sales of 2024. Understanding the land cost gives buyers valuable insight into the pricing and value proposition of this mega development.

> **Key deal snapshot:** Thomson View was sold for S$810 million — approximately $1,178 psf per plot ratio (psf ppr). The deal was completed by UOL Group, CapitaLand Development, and Singapore Land Group.

**How the Price Was Reached**

The Thomson View en bloc journey was not straightforward. The collective sale committee initially set a reserve price of S$918 million. After the site failed to attract bids, the owners reset expectations to S$808 million. The final winning bid of S$810 million — submitted by the UOL-led joint venture — was accepted in 2024, making it the year's largest en bloc transaction.

**Land Cost Breakdown**

*Land Area Analysis*
- Total land area: 504,314 sqft
- Plot ratio: 2.1
- Gross floor area (GFA): ~1,059,059 sqft
- Effective land cost: ~S$765 psf of GFA

*Estimated Development Cost*
- Land cost: ~S$765 psf of GFA
- Construction + professional fees: ~S$400–S$500 psf
- Total development cost: ~S$1,165–S$1,265 psf of GFA
- Breakeven (including financing, marketing, profit): Land + construction + 15–20%

> **What this means for buyers:** The S$1,178 psf ppr land cost is competitive for District 20. Comparable new launch land bids in the Thomson/Lentor area have ranged from S$1,000 to S$1,300+ psf ppr.

**FAQ**

*How much did each Thomson View owner get from the en bloc?*
Between S$2.2 million and S$4.9 million per unit, depending on size.

*Was the S$810 million price considered high or low?*
Approximately 11.8% below the original S$918 million reserve. Analysts considered it reasonable for a prime D20 location.

*What is psf ppr and why does it matter?*
It stands for "per square foot per plot ratio" — standardising land cost across sites of different sizes and densities for fair comparison.

*Will the land cost affect Thomson Reserve launch prices?*
Yes, it's a key factor. But final pricing also depends on construction costs, market conditions at launch (targeted October 2026), and the developers' pricing strategy.

*How does this compare to nearby Lentor Hills sites?*
Lentor Hills land bids have ranged from ~S$1,000 to S$1,200 psf ppr. Thomson Reserve's S$1,178 psf ppr is within the same range, with the advantage of a more established Upper Thomson location.

Cross Island Line Phase 3 Confirmed — What It Means for Thomson Reserve & Bright Hill

Published July 31, 2026 · District 20, Upper Thomson · By Jet Lee, PropNex Realty (CEA Reg No. R007613B)

On July 31, 2026, Transport Minister Jeffrey Siow announced the third and final phase of the Cross Island Line (CRL) — four new stations (CR20–CR23) with interchanges at Jurong Pier and Gul Circle, completing Singapore's longest fully underground rail line at over 65km from Changi to Tuas.

For Thomson Reserve buyers, the headline isn't Phase 3 itself — it's what the full CRL picture confirms about Bright Hill, the Phase 1 terminus that sits right beside Upper Thomson.

🚇 Bright Hill — A Rare Double-Line Node

Bright Hill (CR12) is the eastern terminus of CRL Phase 1 (targeted 2030) and an interchange with the Thomson-East Coast Line (TEL). Thomson Reserve's location near Upper Thomson MRT (TEL) places it minutes from one of Singapore's newest interchange hubs.

Why Bright Hill Changes the Upper Thomson Story

Upper Thomson is no longer just a TEL stop. With Bright Hill as the CRL Phase 1 terminus, the Upper Thomson area becomes a gateway between the TEL's north-south spine and the CRL's cross-island spine.

  • One-seat rides across the island: From 2030, residents can board the CRL at Bright Hill and travel toward Ang Mo Kio, Hougang, Punggol, Loyang and Changi — without going through the city.
  • Network resilience: Two lines and an interchange nearby mean more route options and fewer single points of failure during disruptions.
  • Employment & lifestyle reach: Direct access to the Punggol Digital District and the eastern growth corridor, alongside existing TEL access to Orchard and the CBD.
  • Confirmed, not speculative: Phase 1 is under construction. Phase 3's confirmation this week closes the loop on the entire 65km line, removing long-term uncertainty for buyers weighing the area's connectivity.
💡 The Big Picture With CRL Phase 1 (2030) at Bright Hill and Phase 2 (2032) extending west, Upper Thomson sits at the hinge of the new cross-island network — while already enjoying TEL access today. That combination — existing line now, new interchange soon — is exactly the profile that historically supports both owner-occupier demand and rental appeal.

What This Means for Thomson Reserve Buyers

  • Transport upside is real and near-term: Bright Hill CRL opens in 2030 — within the typical holding period of a new launch buyer.
  • Wider buyer pool: Workers and families across the north-east and east gain a reason to consider Upper Thomson.
  • Rental resilience: Interchange proximity consistently broadens tenant demand in Singapore's leasing market.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bright Hill station confirmed on the CRL?

Yes. Bright Hill (CR12) is the Phase 1 terminus, under construction, targeted for 2030, and will interchange with the Thomson-East Coast Line.

How far is Thomson Reserve from Bright Hill MRT?

Thomson Reserve is located in the Upper Thomson area, near Upper Thomson MRT on the TEL. Bright Hill interchange is a short distance away, connecting the TEL and CRL networks.

When does the full Cross Island Line open?

Phase 1 in 2030, the Pasir Ris–Punggol extension in 2032, Phase 2 in 2032, and Phase 3 in the late 2030s (construction starts 2027).

Does Phase 3 affect Upper Thomson directly?

Not directly — Phase 3 is in the west. But it confirms the full 65km line, which strengthens the network case for every interchange on the line, including Bright Hill.

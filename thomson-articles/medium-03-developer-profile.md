*Published June 2026 · Three blue-chip developers, one mega project*

Thomson Reserve is a rare joint venture between three of Singapore's most established developers: UOL Group, CapitaLand Development, and Singapore Land Group. Each brings decades of experience and a portfolio of award-winning developments.

**UOL Group**

*Founded: 1963 · Listed on SGX*

UOL Group is one of Singapore's largest listed property companies. They own a significant land bank and have a reputation for delivering well-designed homes with strong capital appreciation.

Notable past projects:
- **Parc Clematis** — Mega development with 1,468 units in District 5
- **The Tre Ver** — Award-winning project along Potong Pasir
- **Principal Garden** — Premium condominium along Prince Charles Crescent
- **Pan Pacific Orchard** — UOL's flagship hotel
- **Clavon** — Successful new launch at Clementi (joint venture with SingLand)

UOL also owns Pan Pacific Hotels & Resorts and PARKROYAL Hotels.

**CapitaLand Development**

*Founded: 2000 · One of Asia's largest real estate groups*

CapitaLand Development (CLD) is the development arm of CapitaLand Group. They've built some of Singapore's most iconic projects:

- **The Sail @ Marina Bay** — Iconic twin-tower development
- **Reflections at Keppel Bay** — Award-winning luxury condo by Daniel Libeskind
- **d'Leedon** — Mega condo with architecture by Zaha Hadid
- **The Interlace** — Won World Building of the Year
- **Jewel Changi Airport** — Iconic airport lifestyle hub

**Singapore Land Group (SingLand)**

*Founded: 1968 · Listed on SGX*

SingLand is a major real estate player with a strong portfolio of commercial and residential properties. UOL Group holds a controlling stake.

Notable projects:
- **Clavon** — Joint development with UOL at Clementi
- **8 St Thomas** — Premium freehold project in Orchard vicinity
- **SGX Centre, One George Street, UIC Building** — Commercial properties

**Why This Joint Venture Matters**

It is uncommon to see three developers of this calibre collaborate on a single residential project. Each brings complementary strengths:

- **UOL** — Design excellence and residential expertise
- **CapitaLand** — Global best practices and iconic design capability
- **SingLand** — Prime location development and long-term asset management

For buyers, this means a project backed by three of Singapore's strongest balance sheets — reducing construction risk and ensuring quality delivery.

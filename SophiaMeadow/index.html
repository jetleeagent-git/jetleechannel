Sophia Meadow:

Sophia Meadows is located in the prime District 9, nestled within the upscale Mount Sophia neighbourhood. This area is known for its blend of historical charm and modern conveniences, offering a peaceful residential environment close to the city center. Residents can enjoy easy access to nearby cultural landmarks, green spaces like Fort Canning Park, and a variety of dining and lifestyle options along nearby Orchard Road and River Valley.

In terms of connectivity, 132 Sophia Road is well served by public transport, with the Somerset MRT Station (North-South Line) and Fort Canning MRT Station (Downtown Line) both within a short distance, providing seamless links to the Central Business District and other key parts of Singapore. The location also benefits from proximity to major arterial roads such as Orchard Road and Clemenceau Avenue, facilitating convenient travel by car.

Families will appreciate the close proximity to reputable educational institutions including the upcoming St. Margaret’s Primary School, as well as other well-established schools in the vicinity. Overall, 132 Sophia Road offers a tranquil yet accessible urban setting for residents seeking to live near the heart of the city.

Project Overview

A Grand Vision in D15

It is well known that land in Singapore is scarce and extremely valuable. Hence, imagine having a mega development that stretches across more than 270,000 sqft and is located right smack in the central region, District 15. You can have the best of both worlds with ultimate convenience, exclusivity and spacious living space.

Presenting Grand Dunman, a luxurious mega-development featuring 6 grand residential blocks consisting of a total of 1,008 units. This development has a dynamic range of 1 to 5-bedroom unit types, with penthouse and dual-key options, ideal for any lifestyle or family configuration.

Developed by esteemed developers SingHaiyi and joint ventured by CSC Land Group, who has a profound wealth of experience in both residential & commercial sectors with a shining portfolio to show for it.

Situated in the private landed enclave along Dunman Road, residents can easily access Dakota MRT station which is only a minute walk away. Convenience is at the heart of Grand Dunman as it only takes about a 7-minute drive to get to Suntec City and the Central Business District.

What is Grand Dunman? ▼
Grand Dunman is a luxurious mega-development at 10 Dunman Road, District 15. It features 6 grand residential blocks with 1,008 units, offering a range of 1 to 5-bedroom units, penthouse and dual-key options. Developed by SingHaiyi and CSC Land Group.
What is the tenure of Grand Dunman? ▼
Grand Dunman is a 99-Year Leasehold development.
Which MRT stations are nearby? ▼
Dakota MRT (Circle Line) is just 210m away — approximately a 1-minute walk. Mountbatten MRT (970m) and Paya Lebar MRT (1,100m) are also within walking distance. The Circle Line, East West Line, and Thomson East Coast Line serve the area.
What unit types are available? ▼
Grand Dunman offers 1 Bedroom (Luxury), 1 Bedroom + Study (Luxury), 5 Bedroom (Grand), and Penthouse (Grand) units. The full unit mix includes 1, 2, 3, 4, 5-bedroom and penthouse layouts.
Who is the developer? ▼
Grand Dunman is developed by SingHaiyi and joint ventured by CSC Land Group — both esteemed developers with profound wealth of experience in residential & commercial sectors.
When is the estimated TOP? ▼
The estimated completion date for Grand Dunman is 2028. The estimated TOP was 31 December 2031 per latest records.

The Orie Overview

The Orie is a 777 units development jointly developed by renowned CDL, Frasers Property and Sekisui House, featuring 2 Towers of 40-storey with units types ranging from 1+Study to 5-Bedrooms. The towers are oriented in the north-south direction; and the higher floors boast unparalleled panoramic views all around.

Built on almost 170,000 sqft of land, it is possibly the largest private residential development in Toa Payoh.

The Orie:

A Canvas To Create The Colours Of Your Life

The Orie is situated in District 12, an area that includes the mature estates of Toa Payoh, Serangoon, and Balestier. Toa Payoh, Singapore’s second satellite town developed by the Housing Development Board, has undergone extensive rejuvenation and is now a vibrant hub offering a blend of residential and commercial amenities. The neighbourhood is known for its well-established infrastructure, including HDB Hub, which integrates shopping, dining, and an air-conditioned bus interchange, making daily conveniences easily accessible to residents.

Accessibility to The Orie is well served by nearby MRT stations, namely Braddell (NS18) and Toa Payoh (NS19) on the North-South Line, connecting the district efficiently to the city centre and other parts of Singapore. These MRT stations are complemented by comprehensive bus services that link Toa Payoh with neighbouring towns and key commercial zones. Major expressways like the Pan-Island Expressway (PIE) further enhance connectivity for motorists residing in the area.

District 12’s appeal is also enhanced by a good mix of recreational and community facilities. Residents enjoy proximity to amenities such as Toa Payoh Stadium, Sports Hall, and Swimming Complex, which support active lifestyles. The area also benefits from green spaces and is connected by a network of park connectors that provide pleasant routes for walking and cycling. As part of the Central Region, District 12 combines mature urban living with convenient access to Singapore’s key commercial and cultural precincts.

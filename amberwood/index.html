King Albert Park MRT Interchange

From Downtown Line station to a two-line interchange — what the Cross Island Line means for Amberwood.

A New Interchange on Your Doorstep

King Albert Park is currently served by the Downtown Line (DT6). Under the Cross Island Line Phase 2 (CRL2), it will become a fully-fledged Downtown Line ↔ Cross Island Line interchange — connecting Upper Thomson, Turf City, West Coast and beyond, with direct links across the island.

The CRL2 design-and-build contract for the KAP station was awarded at approximately S$447 million, with construction commencing in Q1 2024. The new CRL exit is planned within the Holland Plain precinct — the exact entrance positions will be confirmed by LTA as works progress.

For Amberwood residents, the future CRL exit sits close to home, while the existing DTL entrances remain within an easy walk.

When a Developer Bets $822M on One Street

Sim Lian Group paid the Singapore Government a combined S$822 million for two adjoining sites in Holland Plain. That is not a bid — it is a declaration.

$1,432 psf ppr
Holland Link (Amberwood) · Awarded Aug 2025 · 22.2% above next bidder · ~212 units
$1,491 psf ppr
Holland Plain Plot 2 · Awarded May 2026 · Sole bid — 4.1% higher than Plot 1 · ~280 units · S$454M

Sim Lian now controls 25% of Holland Plain (2 of 8 parcels). No other developer has a footprint here yet. When the de facto master developer pays more for Plot 2 than Plot 1, it tells you everything about where this precinct is headed.

Buy before the street is obvious.
Two sites secured, Plot 3 (~610 units) queued for bidding December 2026. The buyer’s window is now — before launches, hoardings and visible works make Holland Plain obvious to everyone.

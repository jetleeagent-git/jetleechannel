What Makes an Area a GCB Enclave?

Good Class Bungalows (GCBs) represent the pinnacle of Singapore's landed housing market. They are governed by strict planning guidelines set by the Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA):

  • Minimum land area of 1,400 square metres — roughly 15,070 square feet
  • Maximum of two storeys in height
  • Building footprint cannot exceed 35% of the land area
  • Located only in designated GCB Areas across 39 exclusive zones in Singapore

With only approximately 2,800 GCBs across the entire island, these homes are among the most exclusive residential properties available anywhere in Singapore. They are not merely homes — they are generational assets.

The Old Holland and Brizay Park GCB enclave has long been a preferred address for successful business founders. This is not speculation — it is public record.

  • Dora Hoan, co-founder of Best World International

These are individuals who built multi-million dollar enterprises from the ground up. Their choice of residence speaks volumes about what this area offers to those who value both success and quality of life.

Why Do Successful Families Choose This Enclave?

1. Privacy

GCB enclaves are among the lowest-density residential areas in Singapore. With generous land plots and wide setbacks, homes are naturally separated from one another. For high-profile individuals and families, this privacy is invaluable.

2. Greenery

Nestled near the Rail Corridor and the Bukit Timah Nature Reserve, this enclave offers tree-lined streets and lush surroundings that are increasingly rare in urban Singapore. The Holland Plain precinct itself spans 34 hectares and incorporates significant greenery.

3. Prestige

District 10 is one of Singapore's most prestigious postal districts. An address in the GCB enclave carries a cachet that transcends market cycles. It signals success — quietly and unmistakably.

4. Good Schools

This area is within 1km of Methodist Girls' School (MGS) and close to other elite institutions including Raffles Girls' Primary School (RGPS), Nanyang Girls' High School, Henry Park Primary School, and Hwa Chong Institution. For families, proximity to top schools is a significant advantage.

5. Low Density

The GCB planning controls ensure that this neighbourhood will remain low-density indefinitely. No towering condos, no crowded streets — just spacious, tranquil living that only a handful of addresses in Singapore can offer.

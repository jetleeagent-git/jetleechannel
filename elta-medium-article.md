## Location & Connectivity — Clementi's Best Address

ELTA sits right in the heart of Clementi, one of Singapore's most established and liveable neighbourhoods.

### MRT Access 🚇
A short distance to **Clementi MRT Interchange** (East-West Line) — direct trains to the CBD, Orchard, Jurong East, and even Changi Airport. The interchange also connects to a massive bus network.

### Major Expressways 🚗
- **AYE (Ayer Rajah Expressway)** — ~15–20 mins to CBD
- **PIE (Pan Island Expressway)** — links east, north, and west Singapore

### Bus Network 🚌
Multiple bus routes run along Clementi Avenue 1, with Clementi Bus Interchange just minutes away for even more connectivity.

## Why Jurong Lake District Matters Now

The Jurong Lake District (JLD) is Singapore's designated **second CBD**. The government has committed billions in infrastructure:

- **Jurong Region Line** — Opening from 2027, connecting JLD to NTU, Tengah, and Choa Chu Kang
- **Jurong East Integrated Transport Hub** — One of Singapore's largest transport interchanges
- **Jurong Lake Gardens** — Singapore's third national garden, 90 hectares of green space
- **JLD commercial expansion** — Targeting 100,000 new jobs in the corridor

Lakeside MRT (EW26) sits at the southern edge of JLD, one stop from Jurong East interchange. From Lucerne Grand:

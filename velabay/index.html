Project Overview

Luxury Living at Bayshore

Discover the refined coastal-inspired living experience at Vela Bay — a stunning new 515-unit condominium nestled in the heart of District 16, Bayshore. Designed for modern families seeking comfort, convenience, and elegance.

Vela Bay offers a curated selection of 1-bedroom to 4-bedroom layouts, each thoughtfully designed to maximise space, natural light, and functionality. With SingHaiyi Group's renowned craftsmanship, every detail speaks to quality living.

Located at 1,3 Bayshore Walk, this 99-year leasehold development sits on a generous 10,497 sqm site, offering residents ample facilities, lush landscaping, and a true sense of community.

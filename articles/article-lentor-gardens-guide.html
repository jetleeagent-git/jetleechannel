The Lentor Hills Story

Lentor Hills is a government-planned housing precinct built around Lentor MRT station on the Thomson-East Coast Line (TEL). It's part of the broader plan to develop the North-South corridor into a liveable, well-connected residential belt. Think of it as what Bishan was in the 1990s — a greenfield precinct that becomes a sought-after address as infrastructure matures.

The TEL is a game-changer for this part of Singapore. Lentor MRT (TE5) puts you at Orchard in 15 minutes, Stevens in 10, and Woodlands in 8. The TEL also connects directly to the Marina Bay financial district, making Lentor viable for CBD workers who want landed-proximate living without landed prices.

Why CDL-Hong Leong Matters

Both CDL and Hong Leong Holdings are among Singapore's most reputable developers. A joint venture between them signals confidence in the Lentor Hills precinct. For buyers, the CDL brand means quality construction, thoughtful layouts, and reliable TOP timelines.

What Makes Lentor Hills Different

  • Master-planned precinct — not piecemeal development, but a coordinated estate with schools, parks, and amenities planned from day one
  • Natural greenery — bordering the Central Catchment Nature Reserve and Lower Peirce Reservoir
  • Competitive pricing vs. AMK/Bishan — Lentor offers newer infrastructure at more accessible entry prices than established neighbouring estates
  • Improved connectivity — the TEL continues opening in phases, bringing more direct links each year

Future Outlook

Land bids in Lentor Hills have been healthy — developers see the potential. As more projects complete and the estate matures, the area will establish itself as a genuine alternative to Bishan and Ang Mo Kio for families wanting newer stock. The green buffer and TEL connectivity are unique selling points that comparable OCR estates don't offer.

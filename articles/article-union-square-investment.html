Union Square Investment Guide — Why D15's Jalan Tua Kong Location Works

Updated July 16, 2026 · by Jet Lee

Union Square at 10 & 15 Jalan Tua Kong in District 15 is an interesting freehold project by Evelyn Tan Investments and AAA Holdings. With 95 units in a boutique development and walking distance to Dakota MRT, it sits in one of Singapore's most beloved residential enclaves — the Katong-Joo Chiat-East Coast belt. Here's my breakdown.

Project Snapshot

📍 Address: 10 & 15 Jalan Tua Kong, D15

🏗️ Developer: Evelyn Tan Investments & AAA Holdings

🏠 Units: 95

📐 Tenure: Freehold

🚇 MRT: Dakota (CC8) — ~8 min walk

🌊 Key Landmarks: Katong Shopping Centre, East Coast Park, Parkway Parade

Location Deep-Dive

Jalan Tua Kong sits in a quiet residential pocket just off Old Airport Road. The area is mature, established, and sought-after — these are the kind of streets where homeowners don't sell often, and when they do, buyers queue up.

Dakota MRT (CC8) on the Circle Line gives you direct access to Paya Lebar, Marymount, and Botanic Gardens. One stop to Paya Lebar for the Paya Lebar Quarter mall and East-West Line interchange. Two stops to Mountbatten if you're heading to the airport or Expo.

Key Investment Metrics

  • Freehold tenure — increasingly rare in D15 for new launches
  • Boutique size (95 units) — easier to achieve full sell-out, tighter resale supply later
  • Katong-Joo Chiat premium — the area's lifestyle appeal (food, culture, heritage) drives consistent demand
  • East Coast Park 5-min drive — lifestyle factor that attracts families
  • Nearby schools: Kong Hwa School (within 1km), CHIJ Katong (within 1km), Tanjong Katong Girls' School, Victoria School

Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • Freehold in a prime D15 location
  • Boutique development — exclusive feel
  • Quiet street but close to amenities
  • Strong rental demand from East Coast professionals
  • Good school network

⚠️ Watch Out For

  • Boutique = fewer facilities than large condos
  • Dakota MRT is a walk, not doorstep
  • Limited nearby F&B within walking distance
  • Entry pricing — freehold D15 commands premium

Neighbourhood Comparables

Looking at recent freehold new launches in D15, The Atelier at Mountbatten and The Avenir (freehold, D10) set the premium benchmark. Union Square's pricing at Jalan Tua Kong positions it below the Mountbatten corridor but above OCR freeholds — reflecting the genuine value of its location.

For context, older freehold condos along Old Airport Road (e.g., Dunman View, One Amber) have seen steady appreciation of 3–5% CAGR over the past 5 years. Union Square, being a newer product with modern specifications, should perform at least in line with this trend.

Who Should Buy

  • Katong/Joo Chiat lovers — you already know why this area works
  • Freehold buyers who want city proximity — D15 is central without being CBD
  • Investors looking at long-term hold — tight supply + land scarcity = steady appreciation

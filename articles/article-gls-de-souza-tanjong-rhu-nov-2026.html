Two New Residential GLS Sites Open for Tender at De Souza Avenue and Tanjong Rhu

Published 26 November 2026 · Singapore Property News · Original source: 26 November 2026

What happened

On 26 November, URA released residential sites at De Souza Avenue and Tanjong Rhu Close under its second-half 2026 Government Land Sales programme. These sites represent additional potential housing supply, not homes currently available for purchase.

What this could mean for buyers and sellers

The locations offer different choices for buyers considering the Upper Bukit Timah and Tanjong Rhu areas. Future launch pricing will depend on tender outcomes, development costs and market conditions. Buyers comparing existing launches should consider current transaction prices and their own holding period rather than assume that future supply will automatically be cheaper.

Practical takeaway

Compare nearby resale and new-launch alternatives, transport access, unit sizes and total purchase costs. Monitor tender awards before drawing conclusions about future selling prices.

Source: URA · 26 November 2026. Market implications are editorial analysis, not guaranteed outcomes.

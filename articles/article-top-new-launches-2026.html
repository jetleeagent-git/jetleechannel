Key Considerations for New Launch Buyers

1. Sub-Sale Potential

With TOP timelines of 3–4 years (2029–2030), buyers should consider sub-sale exit opportunities if prices appreciate during construction. Projects with strong location factors (MRT-linked, integrated) generally see better sub-sale performance.

2. ABSD Considerations

New launch buyers purchasing as their first property pay the prevailing ABSD rates based on their residency status. If you're buying as an investment (second property), factor in the higher ABSD rates — verify current applicable rates with IRAS before committing.

3. Financing for New Launches

New launch payment schedules are more staggered than resale — you pay progressively as construction milestones are reached. This means you need less upfront cash but must ensure your income can support the progressive payments and eventual mortgage when TOP arrives.

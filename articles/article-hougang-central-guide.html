Hougang Central Condo — Everything You Need to Know About This D19 New Launch

Updated July 16, 2026 · by Jet Lee

Hougang Central Condo sits at the junction of Hougang Avenue 1 and Hougang Avenue 8 in District 19, right next to Hougang MRT and Hougang Hub. It's one of the most anticipated OCR launches for HDB upgraders in the North-East region. Here's what you need to know.

📍 Location: Hougang Ave 1 / Ave 8, D19

🚇 MRT: Hougang (NE14) — doorstep

🏬 Amenities: Hougang Hub (shopping, library, sports hall) next door

📐 Tenure: 99-year Leasehold

🔗 View Hougang Central Project Page →

Why Hougang Works

Hougang Central is one of the most established HDB towns in Singapore. The launch of Hougang Hub in 2009 transformed the area, giving residents a proper town centre with a shopping mall, public library, sports complex, and bus interchange all integrated with the MRT station. A new launch directly adjacent to this hub is a rare opportunity.

The North-East Line (NEL) connects Hougang directly to HarbourFront in under 20 minutes — Little India, Dhoby Ghaut, and Clarke Quay are all a direct ride away. The upcoming Cross Island Line (CRL) with a station at Hougang will add another layer of connectivity when it opens in the 2030s.

Who This Is For

  • HDB upgraders currently in Hougang/Sengkang — this is the most natural upgrade path
  • Families — good school network including Holy Innocents', CHIJ Our Lady of Good Counsel, and Xinmin Primary
  • Investors — Hougang's HDB heartland population creates consistent rental demand for condos
  • NEL commuters — direct train to the city without changes

Investment Outlook

Hougang resale condos have shown steady, if not spectacular, appreciation. What makes this launch interesting is the HDB upgrader demand — the area has a massive HDB population that naturally wants to upgrade to condos without leaving their familiar neighbourhood. The adjacency to Hougang Hub is a genuine lifestyle advantage that younger projects in less mature estates don't have.

HDB Cash Proceeds Going Electronic From 7 Sep 2026 — What Resale Sellers Must Do

Published 19 August 2026 by Jet Lee, Senior Associate Group District Director, PropNex Realty

Key change: From 7 September 2026, HDB resale flat sellers who submit their portion of the resale application on or after this date will receive their cash proceeds via electronic payment directly into their bank account — no more cheques. The move aligns with MAS and ABS requirements for banks to cease processing corporate cheques by end-December 2026.

Why the Change?

HDB is transitioning from corporate cheques to electronic payment for resale flat sellers. The change is in line with the regulatory requirements by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) and the Association of Banks in Singapore (ABS), which require banks to stop processing corporate cheques by end-December 2026.

If you've registered an Intent to Sell for your HDB flat, this affects how you'll receive your money when the sale completes.

Source: Official letter from HDB Resale Department (Terence Chan, Director, Resale Department, Estate Administration & Property Group) — "Transition from Corporate Cheques to Electronic Payment for Resale Flat Sellers".

Which Rule Applies to You?

When you submit your resale application How you receive cash proceeds
Before 7 September 2026 Still by cheque — but deposit it promptly before 31 December 2026, as banks will no longer accept corporate cheques after this date.
On or after 7 September 2026 Electronic payment — credited directly to your bank account by the next working day after your flat sale is completed.
⚠️ If you still receive a cheque: banks will stop accepting corporate cheques after 31 December 2026. Don't let your cheque sit in a drawer — deposit it as soon as you receive it.

What You Need to Do (Applying On or After 7 Sep 2026)

You'll need to provide your bank account details to either HDB or your own solicitor, depending on who you appoint to act for you in the sale:

✅ Appointing HDB to act for you

  • Provide your bank account details via the HDB Flat Portal after submitting your resale application.
  • You'll receive an automated email from HDB with instructions after submitting your application.
  • For privacy, you, as the seller, must enter your bank account details personally through the HDB Flat Portal — don't share your login with anyone.
  • Do this within three calendar days of receiving the notification from HDB to avoid delays in processing your application.

✅ Appointing your own solicitor

  • Provide your bank account details directly to your solicitor.
  • You do not need to submit your bank account details to HDB.

Important Notes

  • Joint ownership: If your flat is held under joint tenancy and the cash proceeds are to be issued in joint names, ensure you have an active joint bank account before submitting your resale application. This helps facilitate timely receipt of the proceeds.
  • Accepted banks: Your bank account must be with one of the three local banks — DBS/POSB, OCBC or UOB — as these banks support automated account verification. This ensures payments are made only to accounts that belong to you.
  • Faster payment: With electronic payment, your cash proceeds will be credited by the next working day after the sale of your flat is completed — typically faster than waiting for and depositing a cheque.

Questions?

If you have any questions, you may write to HDB via HDB InfoWEB > Write to Us.

My Take

This is a good thing for sellers — and worth planning around. Electronic payment means your cash proceeds land in your bank account within a working day of completion, instead of waiting for a cheque to arrive and then clear. For HDB upgraders who are timing their sale to buy a condo, faster cash flow gives you more flexibility on your next move.

The practical takeaway: if your resale application will be submitted on or after 7 September 2026, make sure your bank details are sorted out before you submit — and if you're a joint owner, get that joint account open early. If you're submitting before that date, just remember to deposit any cheque you receive before the end of the year.

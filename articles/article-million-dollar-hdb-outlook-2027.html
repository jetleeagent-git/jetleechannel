Million-Dollar HDB Resales Could Reach 2,800 in 2027

Published 26 November 2026 · Singapore Property News · Original source: 25 November 2026

What happened

In its outlook reported on 25 November, Huttons projects that 2,400 to 2,800 HDB flats could change hands at S$1 million or above in 2027. This is a forecast, not a confirmed transaction count. The agency expects the broader resale market to remain relatively soft.

What this could mean for buyers and sellers

High-value HDB transactions should not be treated as a benchmark for every flat. Location, floor area, condition, remaining lease and nearby amenities can materially affect achievable prices. An owner planning to upgrade should base the purchase budget on comparable completed transactions rather than record-setting deals.

Practical takeaway

Obtain a realistic valuation range and calculate net proceeds before reserving a private home. Allow room for a lower-than-expected sale price.

Source: EdgeProp Singapore, citing Huttons · 25 November 2026. Market implications are editorial analysis, not guaranteed outcomes.

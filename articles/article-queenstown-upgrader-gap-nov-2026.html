Queenstown Offers the Smallest Percentage Price Gap for HDB-to-Condo Upgraders

Published 26 November 2026 · Singapore Property News · Original source: 25 November 2026

What happened

A comparison published on 25 November identifies Queenstown as having the smallest percentage price difference between larger HDB resale flats and mid-sized resale condos across 24 towns. The reported median prices were approximately S$1.26 million for five-room or executive flats and S$2.2 million for resale condos of 1,000–1,399 sq ft.

What this could mean for buyers and sellers

A smaller percentage gap does not necessarily mean an affordable upgrade: the indicative absolute gap is still S$940,000 before transaction costs. Resale condos may offer a lower entry price than nearby new launches, but remaining lease, maintenance costs and renovation requirements need to be weighed carefully.

Practical takeaway

Calculate net HDB sale proceeds after outstanding loans and CPF refunds, then assess available cash, CPF, financing and buyer's stamp duty before committing.

ELTA Clementi — Balance Units, Floor Plans & Pricing Guide

Updated July 16, 2026 · by Jet Lee

ELTA Clementi at 10 & 12 Clementi Avenue 1 is MCL Land and CSC Land's landmark 501-unit development in District 5. With limited balance units still available, here's everything you need to know about what's left, the floor plans, and whether it's worth your time.

Project Snapshot

📍 District: 5 (Clementi)

🏠 Total Units: 501 in two 39-storey towers

📐 Unit Types: 1BR (527 sqft) to 5BR (1,539 sqft)

Why ELTA Sold Well

ELTA launched to strong demand for a few clear reasons. First, Clementi is a proven location with established schools (Nan Hua Primary, NUS High School), direct MRT access, and the Clementi town centre's amenities. Second, MCL Land is a trusted developer with a history of quality projects. Third, the 39-storey towers offer unobstructed skyline views that are rare in the Clementi area.

At launch pricing around $2,233 psf, ELTA was competitively priced against Clementi resale condos like The Clementsi (recent transactions at $1,800–$2,000 psf) and newer launches in neighbouring districts.

Available Floor Plans (Balance Units)

1-Bedroom (527 sqft)

Compact but well-proportioned — suitable for singles or investors targeting the NUS/NUH rental pool. Junior academic staff and medical professionals at NUH are consistent tenants for Clementi-area condos.

1BR + Study (560 sqft)

The study adds flexibility — can be used as a home office or guest space. Popular with young professionals.

2-Bedroom (700–775 sqft)

The sweet spot for couples and small families. Dual-key potential makes this interesting for investors who want owner-occupier flexibility.

2BR + Study (775 sqft)

One of the more sought-after layouts. The extra room is a legitimate second living area without jumping to a 3BR price point.

3-Bedroom (1,012 sqft)

Family-friendly sizing. The 3BR units at ELTA offer practical layouts with decent kitchen and living space — no awkward corners. Best suited for families with 1–2 kids.

4-Bedroom (1,280–1,399 sqft)

Serious family living with generous bedrooms and a proper kitchen. These are limited and tend to move fast when available.

5-Bedroom (1,539 sqft)

The crown jewel. A true 5BR layout in a 39-storey tower with panoramic views. Only a handful available.

Investment Analysis

Rental yield: Clementi consistently delivers 3–4% gross rental yields due to the NUS/NUH tenant base. ELTA's newer finishes command a premium over older condos in the area.

Capital appreciation: The Jurong Lake District transformation (JLD) will benefit Clementi as the next station along the East-West line. As JLD matures, demand for housing in the wider Jurong-Clementi corridor will increase.

Exit strategy: With only 501 units (relatively compact by new-launch standards), supply is limited. Once fully sold, resale supply will be tight — good for sellers.

Who Should Buy ELTA Balance Units?

  • HDB upgraders from Clementi/Jurong — stay in your area without a major lifestyle shift
  • NUS/NUH professionals — the commute convenience pays for itself
  • Parents buying for kids at NUS — walking distance to one of Singapore's top universities
  • Investors with a 5–10 year horizon — JLD tailwind + tight supply = steady appreciation

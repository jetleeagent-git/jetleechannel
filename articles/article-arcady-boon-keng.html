Arcady at Boon Keng — Why This Freehold D12 Gem Deserves Your Attention

Updated July 16, 2026 · by Jet Lee

Freehold new launches in District 12 are a rare breed. The Arcady at Boon Keng at 2 Saint Barnabas Lane is exactly that — a 172-unit freehold development by a solid consortium (KSH Holdings, SLB Development, and Ho Lee Group) sitting in a location that's quietly well-connected.

Let me tell you why this one's worth a look.

Project Snapshot

📍 Address: 2 Saint Barnabas Lane, D12 (Boon Keng)

🏗️ Developer: KSH Holdings, SLB Development, Ho Lee Group

🏠 Units: 172

📐 Tenure: Freehold

🚇 MRT: Boon Keng (NE9) — 8-min walk

📏 Unit Types: 1BR+Study to 4BR & Penthouse (420–1,485 sqft)

Location Advantages

Saint Barnabas Lane sits in a mature residential enclave just off Serangoon Road. You're walking distance to Boon Keng MRT (NE9), two stops from Little India, three stops from Dhoby Ghaut. The CTE is minutes away by car, putting you at Suntec City in under 10 minutes and the CBD in 12.

The area around Boon Keng is established — you've got your coffee shops, wet market, 24-hour NTUC at Bendemeer, and a growing number of hipster cafes creeping up from the Jalan Besar corridor. It's real Singapore, not a sterile new town.

Freehold — Why It Matters

In Singapore's new launch market, freehold has become the exception rather than the norm. The government's land sale programme overwhelmingly releases 99-year leasehold sites. Freehold land, especially in Districts 8–14, is increasingly fought over at en bloc sales — which means higher land costs for developers and fewer freehold launches each year.

For buyers, freehold means:

  • No lease decay anxiety — you can hold as long as you want
  • Better multi-generational play — passes down to kids without worrying about the lease
  • Stronger resale liquidity — freehold always attracts a premium in the resale market

Unit Mix Highlights

The Arcady offers a sensible range from 1BR+Study (420 sqft) all the way to 4BR Penthouse (1,485 sqft). The sweet spot is probably the 2BR (614 sqft) and 3BR (893 sqft) units — they hit the right balance for families and investors without being too large or too small.

Boutique size (172 units) means lower maintenance fees per unit and a tighter community feel. For investors, limited supply = less competition on resale.

Investment Perspective

Boon Keng is gentrifying. The entire Kallang-Whampoa-Bendemeer triangle has seen significant rejuvenation — new BTO flats, the new Kallang Riverside developments, and the general northward creep of the city fringe. The Arcady is positioned to benefit from this trend.

Rental demand comes from young professionals working in the CBD/Little India area and NUS students at the nearby Bendemeer campus. Resale upside comes from freehold scarcity + area gentrification.

Who Should Consider The Arcady?

  • Freehold seekers on a budget — D12 freehold is more accessible than D9/10/11
  • Investors betting on city fringe gentrification — the Kallang area is transforming
  • HDB upgraders from the area — stay near Serangoon/Toa Payoh without leaving your community
  • Parents buying for kids — within 1km of Bendemeer Primary, St. Andrew's, and Stamford American International

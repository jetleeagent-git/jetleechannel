Suburban New Private Home Sales Fall 65.6% in Q3 2026

Published 26 November 2026 · Singapore Property News · Original source: 24 November 2026

What happened

A Savills report covered on 24 November states that new private home sales in the Outside Central Region declined 65.6% from the preceding quarter to 514 homes in Q3 2026. OCR nevertheless accounted for 43.5% of new-home sales.

What this could mean for buyers and sellers

Transaction volume is not the same as price movement. A quarter with fewer major launches can record weaker sales without equivalent price declines. Elevated land costs and demand from upgraders may support prices, while increased unsold inventory can create more competition between projects.

Practical takeaway

Compare actual launch take-up, remaining unit choices and effective purchase prices against nearby resale alternatives. Avoid making decisions based on sales-volume headlines alone.

Source: Singapore Business Review, citing Savills Singapore · 24 November 2026. Market implications are editorial analysis, not guaranteed outcomes.

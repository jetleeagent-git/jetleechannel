Jurong Lake District Moves Forward with Town Hall Link Tender Award

Published 26 November 2026 · Singapore Property News · Original source: 24 November 2026

What happened

URA announced the Town Hall Link tender award on 24 November. The development has potential gross floor area of 186,139 square metres, including at least 40,000 square metres of offices, up to 1,200 private homes and 44,000 square metres for complementary uses.

What this could mean for buyers and sellers

The award is a concrete planning milestone for Jurong Lake District. A larger concentration of workplaces, housing and amenities could strengthen the area's appeal over time, but the development schedule and actual market response remain uncertain. Additional housing supply can also increase competition for buyers and tenants.

Practical takeaway

For western Singapore property purchases, distinguish existing amenities from future plans. Evaluate current connectivity, entry price, construction timeline and the project's likely competing supply.

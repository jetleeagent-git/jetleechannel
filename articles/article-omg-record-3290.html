One Marina Gardens Notches New Price High of $3,290 psf

Published 29 July 2026 by Jet Lee, Senior Associate Group District Director, PropNex Realty

Quick take: One Marina Gardens, the Kingsford development at Marina South, set a new psf record of $3,290 on 17 June 2026 — the first time a unit there has crossed the $3,200 psf mark. A 1,647 sq ft four-bedder on the 28th floor sold for $5.42 million.
Source: EdgeProp Singapore — One Marina Gardens notches new price high of $3,290 psf

The Record Transaction

On 17 June 2026, a 1,647 sq ft four-bedroom unit at One Marina Gardens on the 28th floor changed hands for approximately $5.42 million — establishing a fresh record of $3,290 psf for the development.

This edges past the project's previous price peak of $3,160 psf, set in January 2026 when a 657 sq ft two-bedder sold for $2.07 million.

Project Snapshot

  • Location: Marina South, District 1 — first residential project in the Marina Gardens precinct
  • Tenure: 99-year leasehold
  • Total Units: 937 across 2 towers
  • Unit Mix: 1BR to 4BR Premium (420 – 1,647 sq ft) — 1BR and 2BR make up over half
  • MRT: Next to upcoming Marina South MRT Station (TEL)
  • Expected TOP: Q3 2028

Sales Performance

One Marina Gardens launched in April 2025 with a 38% take-up rate at an average price of $2,953 psf over launch weekend. As of 30 June 2026:

  • 640 units sold (68% of total 937 units)
  • Average price: ~$2,966 psf
  • Prices have appreciated from launch average to the new record of $3,290 psf — a gain of about 11% from launch

My Take

This $3,290 psf record is significant for a few reasons. First, it shows that buyer confidence in the Marina South precinct remains strong — the location next to Gardens by the Bay and the upcoming Marina South MRT is clearly resonating with buyers willing to pay a premium. Second, the fact that a large four-bedder (not just a small unit) set the record suggests end-user demand rather than pure investor flipping.

For context, the project's average psf has risen from ~$2,953 at launch (April 2025) to ~$2,966 currently. While the headline record of $3,290 psf is impressive, it's important to note that this represents a premium unit on a high floor — the overall average has moved more modestly.

With 68% sold and TOP expected in Q3 2028, there are still ~297 units available for buyers who want to enter Marina South at prices that may look attractive compared to future launches in the same precinct.

Interested in One Marina Gardens?

One Marina Gardens offers a rare opportunity to own in the new Marina South precinct — Singapore's next waterfront residential address. With direct covered linkway to Marina South MRT, minutes from Marina Bay financial district and Gardens by the Bay, it's a compelling option for those who want city-centre living without the CCR price tag of developments closer to Orchard.

### The 5Ps Explained

**1️⃣ Proven Lowest Land Cost (PTE GLS)**
Entering at the lowest possible land cost provides a strong protective buffer for your capital. Hudson Place's land was acquired through GLS at ~$1,037 psf ppr — a disciplined entry that gives the developer room to deliver quality without pushing unsustainable pricing.

**2️⃣ Proven First Mover Advantage**

**3️⃣ Proven Resale Transactions**
The investment is backed by hard data showing strong demand and profitable prices in the surrounding resale market. One-North's existing projects (The Crest, One-North Residences) show healthy resale activity.

**4️⃣ Proven New Launch Transactions**
Pricing is validated by the successful sales performance and benchmark prices set by other recent new launches in the vicinity. Nearby projects like Blossoms by the Park and The Hill @ One-North have set clear pricing benchmarks.

**5️⃣ Proven Exit**
There is a clear, historically supported path to future profitability and demand when it is time for you to sell. One-North's employment density ensures a ready pool of future buyers and tenants.

## 🔒 P — PROTECTED

**Keywords:** *Future-Proof, GFA Harmonization, Liveable Space*

This is where the math gets interesting.

Under the new **GFA Harmonization** rules, buyers no longer pay for non-liveable spaces like air-con ledges, planter boxes, and bay windows. This sounds great — but here's the catch:

**The psf prices of future developments WILL mathematically increase.**

Why? Because developers can't spread their costs across unusable square footage anymore. Every square foot you buy must be liveable — so the base psf has to go up.

By entering the **first phase of Harmonization now**, your entry price is **protected** against these upcoming benchmark hikes.

**Hudson Place** in particular benefits from One-North's **remaining development parcels**. As each new plot launches at Harmonization-inflated psf prices, your Phase 1 entry price looks better and better.

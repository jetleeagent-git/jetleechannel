Presenting Robertson Opus — a premier freehold development by Tang Kok Thye at Unity Street in prestigious District 9 (River Valley / Orchard). Designed by renowned ADDP Architects LLP, this intimate collection of just 42 residences offers an exquisite choice of Suites, 1-Bedroom and 2-Bedroom homes.
Nestled in the vibrant Robertson Quay neighbourhood, residents enjoy unparalleled access to the Orchard shopping belt, the Singapore River dining scene, and three major MRT lines — all within a quiet, leafy enclave.

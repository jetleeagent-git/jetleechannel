Hougang Central & the Cross Island Line — Defu Station (CR9) on Phase 1, Opening 2030

Updated July 31, 2026 · by Jet Lee

On July 31, 2026, LTA confirmed the third and final phase of the Cross Island Line, completing a 65km network from Changi to Tuas. For Hougang, the key fact remains Phase 1 — the 29km stretch under construction now, targeted for 2030 — which includes Defu station (CR9) in the Hougang/Kovan corridor.

📍 Project: Hougang Central (by CapitaLand & UOL)

🏗️ Type: Integrated development in D19

🚇 Today: Hougang MRT (North-East Line) — doorstep

🚇 2030: Defu station (CR9) on CRL Phase 1 — cross-island access

🚇 Also: Kovan MRT (NEL) nearby

What Defu Station (CR9) Adds

Hougang today is served by the North-East Line — excellent for the city and north-east, but cross-town trips to the west or east have always required transfers. The CRL changes that:

Why This Matters for Hougang Central

  • Dual-line catchment: An integrated development beside Hougang MRT (NEL) gains a second rail line nearby — expanding its commuter catchment toward the east and west.
  • Cross-island one-seat rides: Residents reach Bright Hill, Ang Mo Kio, Tampines and Loyang without changing lines — a genuine upgrade for cross-town commuters.
  • Confirmed timeline: Phase 1 is under construction with a 2030 target. The Phase 3 announcement this week confirms the whole 65km network, removing long-term uncertainty.
  • Integrated development appeal: Hougang Central's retail + residential + transport mix becomes even more attractive with two rail lines serving the area.

💡 Investor Takeaway: Hougang is already one of the most established HDB heartlands in Singapore. The CRL's Defu station gives Hougang Central a second-line story — and with Phase 1 under construction, the upgrade is visible, funded, and on a 2030 timeline.

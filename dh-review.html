Dunearn House Review: Can You Really Earn? A Deep Dive into the Investment Thesis

Published 8 July 2026 by Jet Lee, Property Journey Advisor

Quick summary: Dunearn House at Bukit Timah Turf City combines a lower land cost (~$1,410 psf ppr vs nearby GLS at ~$1,625+), efficient GFA Harmonisation layouts, and a long-term ecosystem transformation. With 2BR from S$1.475M, 3BR from S$2.597M, and 4BR from S$3.588M, this project offers compelling value for buyers who understand delayed gratification. Using my SRPU Framework, here's why I believe Dunearn House can earn.

Every new launch claims to be "the next big thing." But experienced property investors know that capital appreciation isn't driven by marketing—it is driven by fundamentals.

So the real question is:

Can Dunearn House Earn?

After studying the land cost, pricing, market positioning, unit mix, and the long-term Turf City transformation, my conclusion is YES—but not because it is simply another Bukit Timah condominium.

Instead, Dunearn House stands out because several powerful investment drivers are working together.

1. Price Protection Starts with Land Cost

One of the strongest investment principles is buying below future replacement cost.

Dunearn House enjoys a significant advantage because its land was acquired at approximately $1,410 psf ppr, following the implementation of GFA Harmonisation. During this period, developers bid more conservatively as they adjusted to the new planning framework.

Since then, nearby Government Land Sales have already demonstrated a clear upward trend:

  • Dunearn House land: ~$1,410 psf ppr
  • Nearby GLS: ~$1,625 psf ppr
  • Other recent sites: $1,556–$1,865 psf ppr

Higher land costs eventually translate into higher selling prices for future projects. This creates what investors call a pricing umbrella.

Buying today means entering the market before surrounding developments establish a higher benchmark. This is one of the strongest forms of price protection available in today's market.

2. GFA Harmonisation Means Buyers Pay for Usable Space

Many older condominiums were designed before GFA Harmonisation. Buyers often paid for:

  • Large air-conditioning ledges
  • Bay windows
  • Planter boxes
  • Long corridors
  • Inefficient layouts

These spaces increased the purchase price without improving everyday living.

Dunearn House is designed entirely under the new GFA Harmonisation guidelines. Every square foot is significantly more efficient. For buyers, this means:

  • Better furniture placement
  • Larger usable living areas
  • More practical bedrooms
  • Better long-term resale appeal

Instead of paying for wasted space, owners are paying for actual liveable space.

4. Why the 3-Bedroom Could Be the Sweet Spot

Among all the unit types, the 3-bedroom configuration may offer the best balance. It appeals to:

  • HDB upgraders
  • Young families
  • Existing private homeowners
  • Multi-generational households

These buyers form one of the largest pools in Singapore's residential market. Combined with a relatively limited supply of quality family-sized homes nearby, the 3-bedroom units could enjoy strong demand from both owner-occupiers and future resale buyers.

5. The 4-Bedroom Opportunity

Perhaps the most interesting proposition is the 4-bedroom unit. Finding a brand-new, efficiently designed 4-bedroom home in Bukit Timah from S$3.588M is becoming increasingly difficult. The layouts are designed under GFA Harmonisation with practical living spaces instead of oversized balconies and wasted corridors. For families looking for long-term own-stay properties, this creates an attractive balance between luxury, functionality, and value.

6. Turf City Is More Than Just a Transformation Story

Many people simply say: "Buy because Turf City is transforming." That explanation is incomplete.

The real opportunity lies in understanding Ecosystem Transformation.

Over the next decade and beyond, Turf City is expected to evolve into a complete township with:

  • New residential precincts
  • Parks and green spaces
  • Transport improvements
  • Retail and lifestyle amenities
  • Schools
  • Healthcare facilities
  • Community spaces

When these pieces come together, they create an ecosystem that supports sustainable housing demand rather than short-term speculation. History has shown that areas undergoing complete ecosystem transformation often enjoy stronger long-term capital appreciation than locations driven by a single catalyst.

7. The Future HDB Upgrader Ecosystem

One particularly interesting aspect of the Turf City Master Plan is the introduction of HDB housing.

Some buyers worry that HDBs may dilute the prestige of Bukit Timah. I see it differently. I believe this creates one of Singapore's strongest long-term property ecosystems.

Here's why:

First-time homeowners purchase HDB flats within Turf City. Over the next 10 years, they build equity while enjoying the convenience and lifestyle of the Bukit Timah area. When they eventually sell their HDB flats, many will naturally look to upgrade to nearby private condominiums rather than relocate elsewhere.

This creates a continuous pipeline of future upgrader demand. Instead of relying purely on outside buyers, Dunearn House could eventually benefit from a growing community of homeowners who already want to remain within Turf City. This is what I call the Capital-Building Ecosystem. It creates sustainable housing demand rather than relying on speculation.

8. Does 99-Year Leasehold Matter?

Some buyers hesitate because Dunearn House is a 99-year leasehold in a traditionally freehold enclave. However, successful property investing is rarely determined by tenure alone.

Instead, long-term performance depends on:

  • Entry price
  • Replacement cost
  • Future affordability
  • Buyer demand
  • Surrounding developments
  • Exit opportunities

A well-priced 99-year development with strong fundamentals can outperform many freehold properties over a 5- to 15-year holding period. Today's buyers are looking for value, affordability and quality—not just tenure.

9. My SRPU Investment Framework

Every project I analyse goes through my SRPU Framework:

S – Safe Entry

Dunearn House enjoys one of the lowest land costs among upcoming nearby developments. Buying before surrounding land prices reset higher provides meaningful price protection.

R – Calculated Risk

The Turf City transformation is a long-term story. Buyers should not expect overnight appreciation. Instead, they should be prepared to enjoy gradual wealth creation as the township matures.

P – Protection

Lower land acquisition cost + efficient GFA Harmonisation layouts + strong Bukit Timah address + limited supply of quality family-sized homes + future developments launching at higher prices = strong downside protection.

U – Upside

Turf City Ecosystem Transformation + future higher-priced GLS + efficient layouts + scarcity of larger family units + potential future HDB upgrader demand — multiple structural drivers working together.

In my opinion, YES.

Not because it is simply another Bukit Timah condominium. But because it combines:

  • Lower land acquisition cost — price protection from day one

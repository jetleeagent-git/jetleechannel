Project Overview

A New Chapter
at Clarke Quay

Union Square Residences is a brand new 99-year leasehold mixed development at 20 Havelock Road, District 02, developed by City Developments Limited (CDL). Designed by award-winning architectural firm Aedas, the 40-storey residential tower is the centrepiece of the Union Square precinct — connecting Clarke Quay riverfront with the CBD fringe.

The land was formerly occupied by Central Square and Central Mall, acquired by CDL in Q1 2022 for $313.2 million. The full development totals 735,500 sq ft and includes Grade A offices, retail, co-living and residential.

The Developer

City Developments Limited

CDL
Established 1963 · SGX Listed

City Developments Limited (CDL) is one of Singapore's largest property companies and a global real estate operating company. With operations in 29 countries and regions, CDL has developed over 50,000 homes and a network of iconic projects across residential, commercial, hospitality and retail.

Known for landmark developments like South Beach, The Sail @ Marina Bay, and Boulevard 88, CDL consistently delivers high-quality, architecturally significant residences that appreciate in value over time.

Union Square Residences is part of CDL's ambitious Union Square mixed-use precinct — a landmark redevelopment of the former Central Mall and Central Square sites under the URA's Strategic Development Incentive (SDI) Scheme.

Other recent CDL launches include The Orie at Lorong 1 Toa Payoh, Zyon Grand at Zion Road, and Lucerne Grand at Jurong — all testament to CDL's consistent quality and design excellence.

Common Questions

What is Union Square Residences?+
Union Square Residences is a brand new 99-year leasehold mixed development at 20 Havelock Road, District 02, developed by CDL Libra Pte Ltd (CDL Group). It features 366 luxury residential units in a 40-storey tower, forming part of the larger Union Square precinct which also includes Grade A offices, co-living, and retail.
Who is the developer?+
City Developments Limited (CDL), established in 1963 and SGX-listed, is one of Singapore's most reputable developers with a track record of over 50,000 homes built across 29 countries.
Which MRT stations are nearby?+
Outram Park MRT interchange (EWL/NEL/TEL) is within walking distance, along with Clarke Quay MRT (NEL) and Fort Canning MRT (DTL). This gives residents access to three MRT lines for seamless island-wide travel.
What is the expected TOP for Union Square Residences?+
The expected Temporary Occupation Permit (TOP) for Union Square Residences is 2031, with the 99-year leasehold commencing from October 2024.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Hudson Place Residences

Where is Hudson Place Residences located?
Hudson Place is at Media Circle Parcel A, beside One-North MRT Station, District 5, Singapore. It sits within one of Singapore's most dynamic innovation precincts.
What unit types does Hudson Place offer?
Hudson Place offers 2BR Premium (646 sqft), 2BR+Study (689 sqft), 3BR (893-1,055 sqft), 4BR (1,152-1,432 sqft), and Penthouses with unblocked city-fringe views.
Who is the developer of Hudson Place?
A joint venture between Qingjian Realty (known for innovative smart living concepts) and Forsea Holdings (global investment and development expertise). Both bring strong credentials to the project.
When is the estimated TOP?
Estimated Temporary Occupation Permit (TOP) is 2029.
How far is Hudson Place from One-North MRT?
It is right beside One-North MRT station on the Circle Line. You can also walk to Buona Vista interchange for East-West Line connections. Major expressways AYE and PIE are within easy reach.
What schools are near Hudson Place?
New Town Primary School, Queenstown Primary School, Fairfield Methodist School, and Tanglin Trust School (533m) are all nearby. Tertiary institutions include NUS, Singapore Polytechnic, and INSEAD — making this a strong education belt location.
Is Hudson Place good for investment?
Yes. It serves both own-stay and investment demand. The location near one-north tech hub, INSEAD, and Tanglin Trust School creates multiple tenant pools — tech professionals, expats, and students — reducing vacancy risk.
What is the land price for this site?
The consortium led by Qingjian and Forsea acquired the site at approximately $1,037 psf ppr. For context, the nearby Dover Drive GLS site was won at $1,556 psf ppr — suggesting early-entry pricing for Hudson Place buyers.
What amenities are nearby?
The Star Vista, Rochester Mall, Queensway Shopping Centre, and Anchorpoint. Cafés and creative studios around one-north and Portsdown Road add character to daily living.

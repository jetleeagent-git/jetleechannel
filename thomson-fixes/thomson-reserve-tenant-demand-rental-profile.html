Who Will Rent Thomson Reserve? Tenant Profile & Rental Demand Explained

Published July 2026 · District 20, Upper Thomson · By Jet Lee, PropNex Realty (CEA Reg No. R007613B)

Most buyers jump straight to "what's the yield?" — but yield is just an output. The real question that determines whether your unit rents fast, rents well, and rents consistently is: who is actually going to want to live here? Get the tenant profile right, and the numbers tend to follow.

Why Tenant Profile Matters More Than the Headline Yield Number

A yield percentage on a spreadsheet doesn't tell you whether your specific unit will lease in 2 weeks or 3 months. Two units in the same building, same yield estimate on paper, can perform very differently depending on whether they're matched to the tenant pool that actually wants that layout, floor, and facing. Understanding who rents in Upper Thomson first helps you buy — and configure — with that tenant in mind.

The Two Core Tenant Pools for Thomson Reserve

1. Working Professionals on the TEL Corridor

The Thomson-East Coast Line gives direct access toward the CBD and Orchard without CCR rental pricing. This tenant profile typically wants efficient 2-bedroom units close to the MRT — prioritises commute time above almost everything else, and signs shorter leases (1–2 years) with higher turnover than family tenants.

2. Families and Expat Households Anchored by Ai Tong School

Thomson Reserve's 1km proximity to Ai Tong School is a genuine draw for this segment — including relocating expatriate families whose companies often prioritise school zone first, budget second. This tenant profile tends to:

  • Commit to longer leases (2+ years), reducing vacancy risk between tenancies
  • Be less price-sensitive than single professionals, provided the school-zone fit is right
  • Prioritise 3-bedroom and larger layouts, and value facilities relevant to children (pools, playgrounds)
  • Value the quieter, greenery-adjacent setting near MacRitchie Reservoir over a more urban, built-up address

What This Means If You're Buying to Rent Out

✓ If Targeting Professionals

  • Favour 2BR layouts near the MRT-facing side
  • Prioritise units closer to the MRT-facing side
  • Expect faster turnover, so factor in re-leasing costs and vacancy gaps

✓ If Targeting Families

  • Favour 3BR+ layouts with practical, family-friendly configurations
  • Longer lease commitments reduce vacancy risk — but tenant pool is smaller and choosier
  • School-zone proximity is the anchor selling point in your listing, not the facilities

Why Limited Nearby Supply Works in Landlords' Favour

Upper Thomson has had relatively few large-scale condo launches historically. Fewer newly completed developments competing for the same tenant pool at the same time generally means less downward pressure on asking rents when a landlord is trying to lease out — though this can shift if several projects in the surrounding area complete around the same period, so it's worth tracking the pipeline as TOP approaches.

My honest take: Don't buy a unit and then figure out who'll rent it — decide who you're renting to first, and let that decide the unit. A 2-bedroom bought for a professional tenant and a 3-bedroom bought for a family tenant are two completely different investment strategies wearing the same building's facade.

FAQ

Who is likely to rent units at Thomson Reserve?

Likely tenant pools include working professionals commuting via the Thomson-East Coast Line, and expatriate or local families drawn to Ai Tong School's catchment and the quieter setting near MacRitchie Reservoir.

Is Upper Thomson a popular area for tenants?

Upper Thomson has historically been an owner-occupier heavy enclave with limited large-scale condo rental supply. New supply entering the area can see steady interest, particularly from family tenants and professionals seeking MRT access without CCR pricing.

Does school proximity affect rental demand at Thomson Reserve?

Yes. The 1km proximity to Ai Tong School is a meaningful draw for family tenants, who often anchor their search around school zones and tend to commit to longer leases.

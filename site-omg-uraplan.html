Over 10,000 New Homes
The Marina South precinct will become a brand-new mixed-use residential neighbourhood with more than 10,000 homes (public and private), creating a genuine residential community instead of a CBD that empties after office hours.
45-Hectare New City District
URA is transforming about 45 hectares of land into a completely new waterfront neighbourhood beside Gardens by the Bay — one of the largest redevelopment areas in central Singapore.

URA Vision

A City Reimagined

🏙️

Mixed-Use Waterfront

Residential towers, commercial spaces, shops, restaurants and childcare all integrated within a walkable waterfront district. A genuine live-work-play community at the heart of Singapore's most iconic skyline.

🚇

Direct MRT Connectivity

Marina South MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line) provides seamless connectivity to the entire island. Covered linkway connects One Marina Gardens directly to the station — 1 stop to Gardens by the Bay, 2 stops to Marina Bay interchange.

🌳

Green & Sustainable

Extensive parks, waterfront promenades, and pedestrian-friendly streets designed with sustainability at the core. Gardens by the Bay is your backyard — one of the world's premier garden attractions.

🛍️

Everything Within Reach

Marina Bay Sands (The Shoppes), Marina Bay Link Mall, Cold Storage at Marina One, and future retail within the precinct. World-class dining, shopping and entertainment at your doorstep.

Singapore's First

10-Minute Neighbourhood

URA has designed Marina South as Singapore's first 10-minute neighbourhood — where residents can reach all daily necessities within a 10-minute walk, reducing dependence on cars and creating a truly walkable community.

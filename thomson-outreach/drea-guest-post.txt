The 20% ABSD Trap — Why Timing Your HDB Sale Matters Before You Visit a Showflat

By Jet Lee, PropNex Realty (CEA Reg No. R007613B)

I've been a property agent for 18 years in Singapore. And in all that time, there's one conversation I've had more times than I can count.

Buyer: "Jet, I'm planning to upgrade to a condo."

Me: "Do you currently own any property?"

Buyer: "Yes, an HDB with my wife."

Me: "Then if you buy a new launch while keeping your HDB, you'll need to pay 20% ABSD upfront. You can claim it back if you sell your HDB within the required timeframe. Alternatively, if you sell your HDB first — before committing to the new launch — you avoid the extra 20% entirely."

Buyer: "Oh... I don't have that kind of cash ready. But let me just go see the showflat first. If I like it, I'll sell and then buy."

And that's exactly where the regret begins.

By the time most buyers decide to sell, the unit they wanted is already gone — taken by someone who was prepared to commit on the spot. It's not just about missing one unit either. It's about missing the entire launch window.

The next project they look at is often priced higher. The one after that, higher still. What started as "let me just look first" turns into years of watching prices climb while they stay stuck on the sidelines, still trying to get their HDB sold, still not financially ready.

What ABSD actually means for upgraders

The Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD) regime in Singapore is designed to cool the market, but it creates a specific challenge for HDB upgraders. If you're a Singapore Citizen buying a second residential property, you pay 20% ABSD on the purchase price. On a $2 million condo, that's $400,000 in ABSD alone.

The silver lining? You can apply for a remission of the ABSD if you sell your first property within six months of completing the second purchase. But that six-month window is tight, and missing it means the 20% becomes permanent.

Three questions every upgrader should answer before visiting a showflat

1. Can I cover 20% ABSD upfront? If yes, you have flexibility. Buy first, sell later, claim remission when the HDB is sold.

2. Can I genuinely sell my HDB within six months? Be realistic about your flat's location, floor, remaining lease, and current market conditions.

3. Am I ready to commit on the spot? If you need to "go back and think," you might lose the unit you wanted. That's not pressure — that's the reality of a competitive new launch market.

The smarter first step

The best time to have this conversation is not after you've fallen in love with a unit. It's before. Sit down, run your numbers, understand your selling timeline, and know your ABSD exposure. That conversation costs nothing, and it could save you years of regret and thousands in missed opportunities.

If you've been thinking about upgrading, start with your numbers — not with a showflat visit. The market rewards those who are ready.

Lucerne Grand & CRL Phase 3 — Jurong Pier & Gul Circle Interchanges Complete the Western Spine

Updated July 31, 2026 · by Jet Lee

On July 31, 2026, the Land Transport Authority confirmed the third and final phase of the Cross Island Line — four new stations (CR20–CR23) with interchanges at Jurong Pier (linking to the Jurong Region Line) and Gul Circle (linking to the East-West Line). Construction begins 2027, with completion in the late 2030s.

For the Jurong Lake District — and buyers at Lucerne Grand at Lakeside (District 22) — this completes the western rail story that Phase 2 (2032) starts.

📍 Address: Lakeside, District 22 (Jurong)

🏗️ Developer: CDL

🏠 Tenure: 99-year leasehold

🚇 Today: Lakeside MRT (EWL)

🚇 2032: CRL Phase 2 — Jurong Lake District station

🚇 Late 2030s: CRL Phase 3 — Jurong Pier (JRL interchange), Gul Circle (EWL)

🚇 Also: Jurong Region Line network across the JLD

Why Phase 3 Matters Even for Lakeside

Phase 3 might be a decade away, but it completes a network that buyers today should understand:

  • Phase 2 (2032): The CRL terminates at the Jurong Lake District — Lucerne Grand's doorstep to a cross-island line connecting to Clementi, Turf City, Bright Hill, Ang Mo Kio, Hougang and Changi.
  • Phase 3 (late 2030s): Extends the line west to Taman Jurong, Jurong Island gateway, Jurong Industrial Estate and Tuas via the Gul Circle EWL interchange — turning the JLD into the hinge of the entire western corridor.
  • Jurong Region Line: CR21's JRL interchange creates a true western regional network — JLD becomes a multi-line hub comparable to Paya Lebar or Bishan.

What This Means for Lucerne Grand Buyers

  • JLD transformation continues: Singapore's second CBD story is backed by the largest rail investment in the west — Phase 2 + Phase 3 + JRL.
  • Employment catchment: Jurong Industrial Estate, Jurong Island and Tuas serve hundreds of thousands of workers — Phase 3 puts Lucerne Grand's residents within a growing commuter shed.
  • One-seat cross-island access: From 2032, a direct ride toward the north-east and east without crossing the city.
  • Pre-MRT window: The JLD CRL station (2032) is already priced in progress; Phase 3 extends the runway of infrastructure-driven demand.

💡 Investor Takeaway: Jurong Lake District is the only region where Singapore is building a second CBD around new rail infrastructure. Lucerne Grand, at Lakeside beside the JLD, sits at the centre of the western CRL story — Phase 2 in 2032, Phase 3 in the late 2030s.

Lucerne Grand in Context

Lucerne Grand by CDL at Lakeside is one of the few new launches with direct access to the Jurong Lake District transformation. With the CRL western spine now fully confirmed, the connectivity thesis for D22 strengthens at every milestone announcement.

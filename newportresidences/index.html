Project Overview

The New Icon of Anson Road

Newport Residences is the latest modern integrated development redeveloped from the former Fuji Xerox Towers, by prestigious developer City Developments Limited (CDL). Rising 41 storeys at 80 Anson Road in the heart of the Central Business District, this freehold mixed development offers 246 exclusive residences.

Set within Tanjong Pagar, Newport Residences sits at the crossroads of Singapore's financial district and heritage enclave — Tanjong Pagar MRT 766m, Maxwell 1km, Shenton Way 1.2km, and Raffles Place 1.8km. A short walk to Amoy Street Food Centre, Lau Pa Sat and the vibrant Duxton Hill dining scene.

Rare sky facilities across multiple levels — Sky Pool, Sky Club, Sky Gourmet, gymnasium, community garden and cascading terraces — with breathtaking views of the CBD skyline, cruise terminal and the sea beyond.

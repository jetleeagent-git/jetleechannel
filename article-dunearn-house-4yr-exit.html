Is Dunearn House Suitable for a 4-Year Exit?

Published 8 July 2026 by Jet Lee, Property Journey Advisor

Quick summary: Can you buy Dunearn House today, sell after four years, and make a profit? The honest answer is that it is possible, but it should not be the primary investment thesis. The real opportunity lies in Turf City's long-term ecosystem transformation over 10–15 years.

One of the most common questions buyers ask is:

"Can I buy today, sell after four years, and make a profit?"

The honest answer is that it is possible, but it should not be the primary investment thesis.

No one can guarantee what the property market will look like four years from now. While future Government Land Sales with higher land costs may support pricing, short-term returns will still depend on overall market conditions, interest rates, buyer demand, and the economic environment.

In my opinion, Dunearn House is not a project that should be purchased purely for a quick four-year exit.

Instead, it is better suited for buyers who understand the value of long-term ecosystem transformation.

The real opportunity lies in buying into Turf City at the beginning of its redevelopment journey and allowing time for the township to mature.

Over the next 10 to 15 years, Turf City is expected to evolve into a complete residential ecosystem with new homes, parks, transport infrastructure, retail, schools, healthcare facilities, and community amenities.

As more phases are completed, surrounding Government Land Sales are launched, and the residential population grows, the neighbourhood is likely to become increasingly established and attractive.

This is where I believe Dunearn House's strongest investment potential lies.

Could you make money after four years?

Yes, it is possible.

However, I would view any profit over that period as a bonus rather than the primary reason for buying.

My investment thesis is based on delayed gratification—allowing the transformation of Turf City to unfold over time and benefiting from the long-term value created by a mature ecosystem.

  • For buyers seeking quick speculative gains, Dunearn House may not be the most suitable choice.
  • For buyers who are prepared to invest with patience and a medium- to long-term horizon, I believe Dunearn House offers a far stronger and more sustainable opportunity for capital appreciation.

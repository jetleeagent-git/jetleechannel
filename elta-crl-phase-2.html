ELTA Clementi & the Cross Island Line — Clementi Becomes a Dual-Line Node by 2032

Updated July 31, 2026 · by Jet Lee

On July 31, 2026, LTA confirmed the final phase of the Cross Island Line (CRL) — but for Clementi, the more relevant news is Phase 2, already under construction and targeted for 2032. The 15km stretch from Turf City to Jurong Lake District includes Clementi MRT and West Coast stations, connecting with the East-West Line at Clementi.

For buyers at ELTA Clementi at Clementi Avenue 1 (District 5), this is the single biggest connectivity upgrade on the horizon: Clementi MRT becomes a dual-line interchange.

What Changes in 2032

Today, Clementi residents travel on the East-West Line. In 2032, the CRL adds a cross-island axis: a one-seat ride from Clementi toward Turf City, Bright Hill, Ang Mo Kio, Hougang and all the way to Changi — without touching the city centre.

Why This Matters for ELTA Buyers

  • Dual-line resilience: Two lines through Clementi means an alternative route during disruptions — a genuine convenience upgrade for daily commuters.
  • West Coast station nearby: The CRL West Coast station (2032) further spreads connectivity across the D5 corridor.
  • Jurong Lake District link: Phase 2 terminates at the JLD, connecting Clementi to Singapore's second CBD and the high-speed regional growth story.
  • Pre-opening window: Buyers entering before 2032 position themselves ahead of the connectivity uplift that typically builds as opening approaches.

💡 Investor Takeaway: Clementi already commands some of the strongest HDB-to-condo upgrading demand in Singapore. Adding a second MRT line to an already-mature transport node is the type of structural upgrade that supports both rental demand and long-term value.

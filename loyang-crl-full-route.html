Cross Island Line Full Route Confirmed — Loyang MRT (CR3) Connects to a 65km Network

Published July 31, 2026 · District 17, Loyang Avenue · By Jet Lee, PropNex Realty (CEA Reg No. R007613B)

On July 31, 2026, the Land Transport Authority confirmed the third and final phase of the Cross Island Line (CRL) — four new stations (CR20–CR23) with interchanges at Jurong Pier and Gul Circle. This completes the entire 65km route from Changi to Tuas, removing any remaining uncertainty about the network that Loyang MRT (CR3) belongs to.

Why This Matters for Loyang

Loyang MRT (CR3) sits on CRL Phase 1 (targeted 2030), the 29km stretch from Aviation Park in Changi to Bright Hill. The Phase 3 announcement confirms the full line — meaning CR3 is not an isolated eastern spur, but part of a complete cross-island spine with 11 interchanges connecting to every major rail line in Singapore.

What the Full Network Means for Loyang Valley Residences

  • One-seat rides across the island: From 2030, CR3 residents can travel toward Tampines, Defu, Bright Hill — and connect onward to the north, west and city through the CRL's 11 interchanges.
  • Confirmed, not planned: Phase 1 is already under construction. The Phase 3 confirmation this week closes the loop on the entire line, giving buyers certainty about the long-term network.
  • Eastern gateway: CR3 is the eastern anchor of Phase 1, minutes from Changi — the same corridor as the upcoming Changi T5 investment story.
  • Resilience: With 11 interchanges across the network, commuters gain alternative routes during disruptions — a factor that matters more as the CRL matures.

Key Takeaway

Loyang Valley Residences sits beside a confirmed, under-construction MRT station (CR3, Phase 1, ~2030) on a now-fully-confirmed 65km line. The pre-MRT window — before the station opens and connectivity is fully priced in — remains the most attractive entry point for buyers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Loyang MRT (CR3) confirmed?

Yes — CR3 is under construction as part of CRL Phase 1, targeted for around 2030. It is not a proposal.

Does the Phase 3 announcement affect Loyang?

It confirms the full 65km line from Changi to Tuas. Loyang benefits indirectly — CR3 becomes part of a complete cross-island network rather than a standalone eastern stretch.

How many interchanges will the CRL have?

11 interchange stations across the full line, connecting to the TEL, EWL, NSL, DTL, JRL and more.

How far is Loyang Valley Residences from CR3?

Loyang Valley Residences is marketed as having doorstep access to Loyang MRT (CR3) along Loyang Avenue. Exact walking distance to the final station entrance will be confirmed once LTA finalises exit locations.

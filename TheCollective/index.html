Project Overview

A New Landmark in the Civic District

The Collective at One Sophia is a brand new mixed development by Singhaiyi, rising on the site of the former Peace Centre / Peace Mansion at the vibrant crossroads of Selegie Road, Middle Road and Sophia Road.

It offers 367 bespoke residences — from Studio to 3-Bedroom Luxury — above a 13-storey office and retail podium, with a spectacular suspended garden by French architect Jean Francois Milou (studioMilou) and ADDP Architects.

Live, work and play under one roof: premium offices and a curated retail mall sit at your doorstep, while Orchard Road, the arts and learning enclave of Bras Basah–Bugis, and the Central Business District are all minutes away. Four MRT stations — Bencoolen, Rochor, Dhoby Ghaut and Bras Basah — are within 700m.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Collective at One Sophia?▼
The Collective at One Sophia is a brand new mixed development by Singhaiyi at 1A & 1B Sophia Road, District 9. It comprises 367 residences across two 19-storey towers above a 13-storey office and retail podium, on the former Peace Centre site.
What is the tenure?▼
99 years commencing from 6 December 2023.
Which MRT stations are nearby?▼
Bencoolen MRT is approximately 450m away, Rochor MRT 500m, Dhoby Ghaut MRT 600m (Singapore's largest interchange — North-South, North-East and Circle Lines), and Bras Basah MRT 700m.
What unit types are available?▼
The development offers Studio, 1-Bedroom Suite, 1-Bedroom Premium, 1-Bedroom + Study, 2-Bedroom Deluxe, 2-Bedroom Deluxe + Study, 2-Bedroom Premium, 2-Bedroom Premium + Study, 3-Bedroom Premium and 3-Bedroom Luxury — 367 residences in total.
Who is the developer and architect?▼
The developer is Sophia Residential Pte. Ltd. (Singhaiyi). The architecture is by ADDP Architects with French architect Jean Francois Milou of studioMilou Singapore, known for the signature suspended garden design.
When is the estimated completion?▼
The estimated date of vacant possession is 31 December 2029 (T.O.P. Dec 2029).
What facilities are available?▼
The SOcial Club, Games Room, Lounge Deck, Azure Spa, Azure Pool, Gymnasium, The Patio, The Playpen (kids play area), The Fitness Zone, Sky Alcove on levels 5, 7, 9, 12, 14 and 19, and One SOcial outdoor lounge at Level 19.

Project Overview

A Distinctive Novena Address

Situated along Bassein Road in the heart of District 11, The Serra Residences introduces a new chapter in Novena — a slender 28-storey tower of just 133 freehold residences, crafted for those who value exclusivity, space and convenience.

With only two to four residences per floor on the lower and mid registers, and exclusive premium 4 & 5-bedroom units with private lifts on the upper levels, The Serra offers the quiet, private living experience of a boutique freehold address.

An eight-minute stroll from Novena MRT, the residence is surrounded by an enviable array of conveniences — from Square 2 and Velocity @ Novena Square to the upcoming 2 Moulmein Road lifestyle hub, plus the medical and education cluster of HealthCity Novena.

Developed by Transurban Properties Pte Ltd, with architecture by award-winning P&T Consultants, interiors by 932 Design, and landscape by ECOPLAN Asia — a design team with a portfolio of iconic projects across Singapore and beyond.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Serra Residences? ▼
The Serra Residences is a boutique freehold development at 7 Bassein Road, District 11. It comprises a single 28-storey tower with 133 units, from 2-Bedroom with Study to 5-Bedroom Premium and Penthouses. Developed by Transurban Properties Pte Ltd.
What is the tenure of The Serra Residences? ▼
The Serra Residences is a Freehold development — a rare and highly sought-after tenure in District 11.
Which MRT stations are nearby? ▼
Novena MRT (North-South Line) is approximately an 8-minute stroll away. Newton, Stevens and Farrer Park MRT stations are also close by, with the CTE and PIE providing easy driving access to the rest of the island.
What unit types are available? ▼
The unit mix includes 2-Bedroom + Study (39 units), 3-Bedroom (39), 4-Bedroom (13), 4-Bedroom / 4-Bedroom + Study (20), 4-Bedroom Premium with Private Lift (10), 5-Bedroom Premium with Private Lift (10) and Penthouses (2) — 133 units in total.
Who is the developer? ▼
The Serra Residences is developed by Transurban Properties Pte Ltd, with architecture by P&T Consultants, interiors by 932 Design Group, and landscape design by ECOPLAN Asia.
What facilities does the development have? ▼
The development features a swimming pool, tennis court, sky terrace on L17, communal facilities on L3, arrival bay, and a 2-storey multi-storey carpark with 106 lots (inclusive of 5 EV lots).
What schools are within 1km of The Serra Residences? ▼
Schools within 1km include St Joseph's Institution Junior, Hong Wen Primary School (Hong Wen School), and Lee Kong Chian School of Medicine (NTU). Anglo-Chinese School (Junior), Farrer Park Primary and Singapore Chinese Girls' School are also nearby.

Thomson Reserve vs Lentor Gardens vs The Arcady — Which 2026 Launch Wins?

Three major Singapore launches in 2026, three very different propositions. Here's how they stack up side-by-side.

Published July 2026 · By Jet Lee, PropNex Realty (CEA Reg No. R007613B)

If you're shopping for a new launch condo in 2026, three names keep coming up: Thomson Reserve (D20, ~1,268 units), Lentor Gardens (D26, 380 units) and The Arcady @ Boon Keng (D12, 99 units).

They couldn't be more different. One is a mega development on the edge of MacRitchie. One is a mid-sized project in the hot Lentor cluster. One is a boutique freehold in the city fringe.

Which one is your right buy? Let's compare every angle.

Location & Connectivity

Thomson Reserve wins on location hands-down — it's the only one in the RCR (Rest of Central Region) at an OCR land cost. Upper Thomson MRT is literally next door, and Bright Hill will become a TEL/CRL interchange in the future. You're also beside MacRitchie Reservoir and Windsor Nature Park — this is a nature-front address.

Lentor Gardens is in the OCR Lentor cluster. It's a nice area with Hillock Park and Lentor MRT, but you're further from the city and the surrounding neighbourhood is still developing. The upside is the Lentor master plan — amenities are coming.

The Arcady @ Boon Keng is RCR on Serangoon Road, D12. Boon Keng MRT on the NEL is walkable. You're close to the city but surrounded by older HDB estates. No nature parks adjacent, but you get city-fringe convenience.

School Priority

This is where Thomson Reserve pulls ahead significantly for families. It's within 1km of Ai Tong School — one of Singapore's most popular SAP primary schools. That alone can justify a premium for families with young children. Neither Lentor Gardens (Anderson Primary, good but less competitive) nor The Arcady (no top-tier primary within 1km) offer the same school advantage.

Investment Outlook

Thomson Reserve — RCR land at $1,178 psf/ppr is unusually cheap. The TEL is still maturing, and the CRL interchange will be a future value catalyst. The mega development format means more resale competition post-TOP, but location quality and scarcity of new launches in Upper Thomson should support pricing.

Lentor Gardens — Frasers/CSC/Sekisui is a strong developer group. The Lentor cluster already has several new launches, which means established comparables and steady demand. But the OCR location means more competition from other OCR projects.

The Arcady @ Boon Keng — Freehold in D12 is rare. Only 99 units means scarcity. But freehold commands a premium (~$2,400+ psf) and it's a relatively small plot. Great for buyers who want freehold city-fringe and don't need mega facilities.

🏆 The Verdict: Who Wins?

For families and long-term investors: Thomson Reserve. The Ai Tong School proximity, RCR location at OCR land cost, and TEL/CRL transport future make it the strongest all-rounder.

For those who prefer a quieter, mid-sized community: Lentor Gardens. 380 units is manageable, the Lentor area is green and planned, and Frasers delivers reliably.

For freehold seekers who want boutique city-fringe: The Arcady. 99 freehold units in D12 don't come around often. But you pay for the privilege and the facilities are limited.

My honest take: Thomson Reserve offers the best risk-adjusted value of the three, especially for upgraders and families. The land cost alone gives it a structural pricing advantage that the other two can't match.

Site Plan Guide

Thomson Reserve Site Plan & Facing Guide

What's confirmed about Thomson Reserve's draft layout and view orientation, and what stack-by-stack facing details UOL Group, CapitaLand Development and Singapore Land Group (SingLand) haven't released yet.

Confirmed — Draft Site Plan

Thomson Reserve's draft site plan includes residential blocks, lagoon-style swimming pools, a clubhouse, BBQ pavilion, kids playzone, gym, pavilion, playground and fitness corner, arranged across the 504,314 sqft Bright Hill Drive site. This layout is subject to authorities' approval and may change before final launch.

Confirmed — View Orientation

Drone-view facing analysis for Level 15–18 references four broad orientations across the development: Nature View, Quiet Facing, Landed View, and City View. These describe the general outlook from upper floors relative to MacRitchie Reservoir Park and Windsor Nature Park (both beside the site), the surrounding low-rise landed housing, and the Upper Thomson skyline.

Nature View
Outlook toward MacRitchie Reservoir Park / Windsor Nature Park
Landed View

Not Yet Released

Which specific stacks, unit types or bedroom counts correspond to each facing — and the final (non-draft) site plan with stack numbers — have not been released as of August 2026. This level of detail is typically confirmed closer to the showflat preview once the site plan is finalised.

Location Context

Thomson Reserve sits directly opposite Thomson Plaza, beside both MacRitchie Reservoir Park and Windsor Nature Park, and near Upper Thomson MRT, Bright Hill MRT and Mayflower MRT on the Thomson–East Coast Line. This location context is confirmed and unlikely to change; it's the unit-level facing detail that's still pending.

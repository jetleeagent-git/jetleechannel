Thomson Reserve 4-Bedroom Units

What's confirmed so far about the 4-Bedroom type at Thomson Reserve, what UOL Group, CapitaLand Development and Singapore Land Group (SingLand) haven't released yet, and how to be first to know when floor plans land.

Confirmed

Thomson Reserve's unit mix spans 2-Bedroom to 5-Bedroom with private lift, across approximately 1,268 residential units (TBC) on a 504,314 sqft site with Plot Ratio 2.1. A 4-Bedroom type sits within that confirmed range.

Not Yet Released

The exact number of 4-Bedroom units, their floor sizes (sqft/sqm), and individual floor plan layouts have not been released by the developer as of August 2026. UOL Group, CapitaLand Development and SingLand have jointly delivered other large-scale residential launches, including Skye At Holland and Parktown Residences. Larger unit types across this consortium's recent projects have generally included private-lift access and premium finishes at the top of the mix — consistent with Thomson Reserve's confirmed 5-Bedroom + Private Lift type.

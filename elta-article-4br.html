Type D1 — 4-Bedroom (Standard)

1,184 sqft · From $3,330,000 · 7 available

The most accessible 4-bedroom entry point. Good-sized bedrooms, a functional layout that works for nuclear families. No wasted space. Starting price under $3.4M makes this the most affordable 4BR at ELTA.

Type D2P — 4-Bedroom Premium

1,313 sqft · From $3,506,000 · 6 available

Premium finishings and a more generous layout than the D1. The additional 129 sqft goes into a larger living and dining area. Limited to just 6 units — the smallest pool of the four, so act fast.

Type D3K — 4-Bedroom Dual Key

1,313 sqft · From $3,396,000 · 20 available

The Dual Key layout gives you a master unit plus a self-contained studio within the same apartment. Ideal for parents living with adult children, or for owners who want a separate rental suite. At 20 available units, this is the most abundant 4BR type and a strong candidate for investment-minded buyers.

Type D4s — 4-Bedroom + Study

1,507 sqft · From $3,686,000 · 22 available

The largest 4BR option. At 1,507 sqft it's bigger than many 5-bedroom apartments in other developments. The dedicated study is a genuine selling point for professionals working from home. With 22 units available, there's still good selection across high and low floors.

Which 4BR Should You Choose?

  • Budget-conscious → Type D1 (1,184 sqft, from $3.33M) — best value per sqft
  • Multi-generational → Type D3K Dual Key (1,313 sqft, from $3.396M) — separate space for parents/tenants
  • Work-from-home → Type D4s + Study (1,507 sqft, from $3.686M) — extra room for an office
  • Premium living → Type D2P Premium (1,313 sqft, from $3.506M) — upgraded finishes, better views

Key Considerations

Location: All 4-bedroom units at ELTA benefit from the same location advantages — 5-minute walk to Clementi MRT, proximity to NUS, Singapore Polytechnic, and one-north. The 39th-storey views from high-floor units are spectacular.

TOP 2030: ELTA is expected to receive TOP in 2030. This gives buyers time for financial planning while benefiting from progressive payment.

Developer Backing: MCL Land (Jardine Matheson / Hongkong Land) and CSC Land Group (China Construction subsidiary, BCA A1 contractor). Both are established names with strong track records.

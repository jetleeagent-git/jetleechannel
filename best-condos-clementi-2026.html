Best Condos in Clementi 2026 — A Complete Buyer's Guide

By Jet Lee · 12 June 2026 · 6 min read

Clementi has long been one of Singapore's most sought-after residential enclaves. Located in District 5, it offers the rare combination of mature estate living, proximity to top schools, excellent transport links, and a thriving innovation corridor anchored by NUS and one-north.

Whether you are a first-time buyer, an HDB upgrader, or an investor looking for rental yield near the knowledge belt, Clementi has something for you. In this guide, I compare the top condominiums in Clementi as of 2026, so you can make an informed decision.

Why Clementi?

District 5 is one of Singapore's most dynamic residential corridors. Here is what makes Clementi stand out:

  • Education hub — Nan Hua Primary, NUS High School, The Japanese School, NUS, and Singapore Polytechnic are all within reach
  • Transport — Clementi MRT (East-West Line), bus interchange, AYE and PIE
  • Mature estate — Clementi Mall, 321 Clementi, markets, food centres, and all amenities
  • Growth corridor — One-north, A*STAR and the biomedical hub continue to drive demand

1. ELTA — The Newest Launch (Our Pick)

Developer: MCL Land & CSC Land Group
Location: 10 & 12 Clementi Avenue 1
Tenure: 99-year leasehold
Units: 501 across two 39-storey towers
Expected TOP: 2029

ELTA is the most recent new launch in Clementi, having opened for sale in February 2025 with a strong 65% take-up on launch weekend. It offers layouts from 1-bedroom-plus-study to 5-bedroom homes, all featuring smart home technology.

Why consider ELTA? It is the freshest product in Clementi with modern specifications. Balance units are still available across all types — from compact 1BR+Study units for investors to spacious 5-bedroom homes for families. Being a new launch, buyers benefit from the progressive payment scheme and lower upfront cash outlay.

Best for: Buyers who want a brand-new home with the latest fittings, smart features, and the longest leasehold tenure in Clementi's current new launch market.

For full details, including floor plans, site plan, and current prices, visit eltasingapore.jetleechannel.sg.

2. Parc Clematis — Resort-Scale Living

Developer: SingHaiyi Group
Location: Jalan Lempeng
Tenure: 99-year leasehold
Units: 1,468

Parc Clematis is a massive development spread across three themed zones — resort, water, and nature. Its 400,000 sqft of facilities include a beach-like pool, tennis courts, function rooms, and lush gardens. The sheer scale means there is always something to do without leaving the compound.

Best for: Families who want resort-style amenities and a vibrant community atmosphere. The size also means good liquidity when reselling.

3. Clavon — Near Top Schools

Developer: UOL Group & UIC
Location: Clementi Avenue 1
Tenure: 99-year leasehold
Units: 640

Clavon is located next to ELTA and shares many of the same locational advantages. Completed in 2023, it is a quality UOL development with solid finishing. Its proximity to Nan Hua Primary School makes it particularly popular among families with young children.

Best for: Families prioritising primary school proximity, or buyers who want a relatively new completed condo without waiting for TOP.

4. The Trilinq — Established Resale Option

Developer: UOL Group & partners
Location: Jalan Lempeng
Tenure: 99-year leasehold
Units: 755

Completed in 2016, The Trilinq features three distinctive tower blocks with resort-style landscaping. Its mature greenery and established condo community are appealing for buyers who want to move in immediately. The development has a good mix of unit sizes and enjoys a quieter position away from the AYE.

Best for: Buyers looking for immediate occupation with proven rental demand and established landscaping.

5. Clementiwoods — Boutique Living

Developer: Fragrance Land
Location: Clementi Avenue 4
Tenure: 99-year leasehold
Units: 241

A smaller, quieter development set further back from the main Clementi corridor. Clementiwoods offers a more intimate living experience with fewer neighbours and a peaceful environment, while still being within walking distance of Clementi MRT.

Best for: Those who prefer a smaller community and a more tranquil setting.

Which One Should You Choose?

You are an investor looking for capital appreciation

ELTA is the obvious choice. As the newest launch with balance units still available, it offers entry at developer pricing with the longest leasehold horizon. The progressive payment scheme also means you can stagger your cash outlay. Plus, being near NUS and one-north, rental demand is supported by a steady flow of professionals and academics.

You have a family with school-going children

Both ELTA and Clavon are within 1km of Nan Hua Primary School. Clavon is already completed if you need to move in soon. ELTA gives you a brand new home by 2029.

You want to move in immediately

Clavon, The Trilinq, or Clementiwoods — all are completed and available on the resale market. Each offers different trade-offs between size, facilities, and pricing.

You want resort-style facilities

Parc Clematis is unmatched in terms of sheer facility space. If you plan to spend a lot of time at home and want everything from a pool to a gym to function rooms, this is your pick.

Final Thoughts

Clementi remains one of the strongest property plays in Singapore's city fringe. With ongoing development in the one-north knowledge corridor and limited new land supply in District 5, property values in this area have consistently shown resilience.

Project Overview

Freehold Land in a Landed Enclave

Belgravia Ace is a freehold strata landed development along Belgravia Drive in Singapore's District 28 (Seletar / Yio Chu Kang), developed by Fairview Developments Pte Ltd. The project comprises 104 units of 3-storey envelope control strata semi-detached houses and 3 units of 3-storey envelope control strata terrace houses — 107 residential homes in total (Phase 3).

Each home features a roof terrace and basement car park, with provision for a swimming pool and communal facilities — a rare blend of landed living and resort-style amenities.

Located within an established landed enclave off Belgravia Drive, the project offers the privacy, space and prestige of landed living — at a time when new freehold strata-landed projects are becoming increasingly scarce under current guidelines.

Developer

Fairview Developments · Tong Eng Group

Fairview Developments Pte Ltd, the developer behind Belgravia Ace, is a subsidiary of Tong Eng Group — a family-owned property developer with a history spanning nearly 60 years and close to 200 acres of land developed across Singapore.

Heritage. The Tong Eng story began in the 1940s, when brothers Teo Thye Chor and Teo Thye Hong arrived in Singapore from Fujian, China, and founded Tong Eng Brothers after World War II. They went on to develop some of Singapore's early landed estates, including Changi Grove, West Sea Garden, Tong Kong Gardens and Tong Eng Heights (now Changi Heights), and later built Tong Eng Building, a 26-storey landmark in the CBD. Today the group is led by its third generation, Group Managing Director Teo Tong Lim.

Award-winning Belgravia series. Belgravia Ace is the final chapter of the celebrated Belgravia series on Belgravia Drive. Its predecessor Belgravia Villas won PropertyGuru's Best Landed Development Award in 2015 and 2017, and the FIABCI Singapore Property Award for Strata Landed in 2019. Belgravia Green took the PropertyGuru Best Landed Development Award in 2018. Belgravia Ace itself won PropertyGuru's Best Strata Landed Development Award, and Tong Eng was named Best Landed Developer in 2021.

A track record you can trust. Other notable Tong Eng projects include Greenbank Park, Green Meadows, Trendale Tower, Stratton Park, Belgravia Park, Serenade Garden, Kew Cottages, Treasure Place, Eminent Plaza and Poshgrove East — spanning landed housing, condominiums, offices and retail.

Project Overview

Held for Generations

Luxus Hills | The Estate is set within the established grounds of Seletar Hills, shaped with care and quiet intention. Once a rubber plantation defined by time, patience and continuity, that same spirit lives on in a neighbourhood designed to feel settled, lasting and familiar — with its own distinctive, timeless identity.

Phase 10 of the Luxus Hills Collection brings 156 brand new 999-year leasehold landed homes: 136 terrace houses, 18 semi-detached houses and 2 detached houses. Every home is designed to balance timeless architecture with the needs of modern family living.

Each residence features four to five levels of living space with a private electric lift, five ensuite bedrooms, outdoor terraces for seamless indoor-outdoor living, and future-ready provisions including rooftop solar panels and EV charging. Premium V-Zug kitchen appliances complete the package.

Green Living

Designed With a Green Vision

Solar energy, simply explained — how your rooftop panels power daily living.

1

Sunlight Captured

Photovoltaic (PV) panels absorb sunlight and convert it into direct current (DC) electricity.

2

Convert for Home Use

An inverter transforms DC into alternating current (AC), making it compatible with household systems.

3

Used Where It Matters

Energy generated powers daily loads such as lifts, appliances, air-conditioning and EV charging.

4

Balanced With the Grid

When solar output is insufficient, the home draws from the grid. Excess energy can be exported back.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Luxus Hills | The Estate (Phase 10)?+
It is Bukit Sembawang Estates' 10th sales phase of the 999-year leasehold Luxus Hills Collection in Seletar Hills — 156 brand new landed homes comprising 136 terrace houses, 18 semi-detached houses and 2 detached houses.
What is the tenure?+
999-year leasehold, commencing 1 January 1879 — effectively a multi-generational legacy asset.
When is TOP?+
Expected T.O.P. in Q4 2028, with expected date of vacant possession by 31 December 2028 and legal completion by 31 December 2031.
What are the home configurations?+
Each home offers four to five levels of living space with a private electric lift, five ensuite bedrooms, outdoor terraces, a carporch for at least 2 cars, rooftop solar panels and EV charging provisions. Premium V-Zug kitchen appliances are included.
Who is the developer?+
Singapore United Estates (Private) Limited — a subsidiary of Bukit Sembawang Estates Limited (BSEL), one of Singapore's longest-established listed developers.
Where is the sales gallery?+
The BSEL Landed Homes Flagship Gallery along Ang Mo Kio Avenue 5. Contact Jet Lee to schedule a private appointment.
How far is it from the MRT?+
Ang Mo Kio MRT (NS16) and Yio Chu Kang MRT (NS15) are within reach, with bus stops along Ang Mo Kio Avenue 5 and Yio Chu Kang Road nearby. CTE, SLE, TPE and PIE provide excellent connectivity.

Pasir Panjang — Where Heritage Meets the Coast

Located at 292 Pasir Panjang Road in District 05, The Hillshore sits in one of Singapore's most character-rich neighbourhoods — surrounded by heritage shophouses, lush greenery, and the sea breeze of the southern coast.

5 Minutes to Haw Par Villa MRT — The Pasir Panjang Location Guide

Location is the one thing you can't change about a home — and The Hillshore's location is quietly exceptional. At 292 Pasir Panjang Road, it's about a 5-minute walk to Haw Par Villa MRT station on the Circle Line, right in the middle of one of Singapore's most characterful neighbourhoods.

The headline: Haw Par Villa MRT (CC25) — 5-minute walk. VivoCity & HarbourFront — minutes by train. one-north & Mapletree Business City — a short drive. Heritage shophouses and the sea — right outside your door.

Getting Around

  • Haw Par Villa MRT (Circle Line) — direct connections to HarbourFront, Labrador Park, Buona Vista and the entire Circle Line loop. Change at Buona Vista for the East-West Line, or at one-north for the future Jurong Region Line interchange nearby.
  • AYE & West Coast Highway — seamless driving to the CBD (approx. 20 min), Jurong, and the west.
  • Bus network — Pasir Panjang Road is a major bus corridor with frequent services along the south coast.

Everyday Life

  • VivoCity — Singapore's largest waterfront mall, minutes away by train or car.
  • HarbourFront Centre & Sentosa — retail, dining, and island escapes on your doorstep.
  • Haw Par Villa — the iconic heritage theme park is literally across the road, a unique piece of Singapore culture.
  • Pasir Panjang Food Centre & hawker options — some of the best local eats in the west.

Work & Business Hubs

  • one-north — Singapore's tech, media and research hub, minutes away.
  • Mapletree Business City — a major office cluster along Alexandra Road.
  • Singapore Science Park — just up the road.
  • CBD — a comfortable commute via AYE or the Circle Line.

The Big Picture: Greater Southern Waterfront

Pasir Panjang sits within the Greater Southern Waterfront transformation — a long-term masterplan that will reshape Singapore's southern coastline with new homes, jobs and leisure spaces. Being early to a district with that kind of trajectory is exactly the kind of long-term thinking freehold buyers appreciate.

Why This Matters for Buyers

Connectivity + character + future growth = a location that holds its value. The Hillshore bundles that with freehold tenure and just 59 units — a genuinely scarce combination in 2026's new-launch landscape.

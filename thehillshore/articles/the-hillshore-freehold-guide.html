Why Freehold Still Matters — The Hillshore's Rare D05 Tenure Explained

The Hillshore 锦绣山庄 at 292 Pasir Panjang Road is one of the quiet standouts of the 2026 new-launch calendar — not because it's the biggest, but because it's the opposite. Just 59 units across two 5-storey blocks, with a word that's becoming increasingly rare in Singapore's launch pipeline: FREEHOLD.

Freehold new launches are scarce. In recent years, the vast majority of new condominium launches have been 99-year leasehold — many of the biggest names of 2025 and 2026 are leasehold projects. That makes The Hillshore part of a small, sought-after category: a genuinely new freehold home in a heritage district.

The quick answer: freehold means the land is owned in perpetuity. There is no lease countdown, no depreciation of the land component over time, and no concern about the value cliff that leasehold properties face as their leases run down.

What Freehold Tenure Actually Gives You

  • No lease decay. A 99-year lease loses land value every year. Freehold land, by contrast, retains its worth — which is why prime freehold properties have historically outperformed in the long run.
  • Multi-generational legacy. A freehold home can be passed down without the clock ticking. For families thinking long-term, that's a genuine structural advantage.
  • Stronger resale demand. In mature districts like Pasir Panjang, freehold homes consistently attract a premium from buyers who value permanence.
  • En bloc optionality. Should collective sale ever come into play, freehold sites are typically more attractive to developers — no lease decay to discount.

Why District 05 Makes It Even More Interesting

Pasir Panjang is one of Singapore's most distinctive addresses — heritage shophouses, the sea breeze along the southern coast, and a relaxed, village-like character that's a world away from the glass towers of the city. It's also increasingly well-connected: Haw Par Villa MRT (Circle Line) is just a 5-minute walk from The Hillshore.

Add the Greater Southern Waterfront transformation plans, one-north and Mapletree Business City minutes away for work, and VivoCity/HarbourFront for retail — and you have a genuinely well-located freehold that appeals to owner-occupiers and investors alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

Singapore real estate questions — answered by an industry insider with 17+ years of experience.

It depends on your budget, timeline, and goals. HDB resale prices are moderating but COV (Cash Over Valuation) has risen to $15K–$35K, up from $5K–$15K in 2025. Condo supply is tightening while foreign demand is returning. If you have around $600K–$1.2M, a resale HDB in a mature estate offers value. If you're looking at $1.5M+, a suburban condo near an MRT station gives better capital appreciation potential. WhatsApp Jet Lee for a personalised comparison.
Cash Over Valuation (COV) is the cash amount you pay above the HDB valuation. It has jumped from $5K–$15K in 2025 to $15K–$35K in 2026. Why? Fewer BTO flats, delayed projects, and cash-rich upgraders competing for move-in-ready units. Tip: always get a valuation report before you start house hunting so you know what cash premium to expect.
CPF usage depends on the property type and remaining lease. For HDB flats: the lease must cover the youngest buyer to age 95. For private condos: minimum 20 years remaining lease at purchase. You can use up to 100% of your Ordinary Account (OA) savings for downpayment and monthly instalments. The Valuation Limit (VL) and Withdrawal Limit rules still apply. Ask Jet Lee for a CPF usage calculation tailored to your situation.
Browse my Projects page for the latest new launches, balance units, and exclusive previews. Or WhatsApp Jet Lee directly to discuss what suits you best.
As of mid-2026, home loan rates are at 2.8%–3.2%, expected to ease to around 2.5% by end of 2026. Fixed-rate packages (2-year or 3-year) remain the most popular for stability. Floating rates (SORA-based) may offer slightly lower initial rates but carry uncertainty. Contact Jet Lee for a free loan comparison from his panel of 8+ banks.
Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD) applies when you sell a residential property within the first 4 years of purchase. Rates: 12% (within 1st year), 8% (after 1 year up to 2 years), 4% (after 2 years up to 3 years). After 3 years, SSD is zero.
BTO is cheaper ($300K–$500K for 4-room) but takes 4–5 years to build. Resale costs more ($500K–$700K+) but you can move in immediately. With ~8,500 BTO flats per year, supply is limited and application rates are competitive. First-timer couples get two BTO chances. My advice: apply for BTO in non-mature estates while monitoring resale prices — if COV drops, resale becomes more attractive.

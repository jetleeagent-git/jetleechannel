5BR vs Landed Property — The Trade-Off

Many buyers compare the E1 to an entry-level landed home. Here's how they stack up:

  • Price: A 1,776 sqft freehold terrace near Clementi would cost $4.5M–$6M+ and need renovation. ELTA E1 from $3.965M is significantly cheaper.
  • Maintenance: Condo = no roof repairs, no termite worries, no garden maintenance. Landed = everything is your problem.
  • Facilities: ELTA offers a 50m lap pool, gym, clubhouse, tennis court, playground — all included in maintenance fees.
  • Security: 24-hour security vs landed where you're on your own.
  • Lease: 99-year vs freehold. The question is whether the lower entry price and convenience outweigh the leasehold trade-off.

Bagnall Haus:

A Freehold Heritage Development

Bagnall Haus is a freehold apartment development situated along Upper East Coast Road in the Bedok area, within District 16. The project is developed by RL Bagnall Pte Ltd and comprises 113 residential units. Residents enjoy convenient transport links, with Sungei Bedok (TE31, DT37) and Bedok South (TE30) MRT stations nearby, providing easy connectivity to other parts of Singapore.

The development sits in a neighbourhood known for its mix of public and private housing, as well as its proximity to natural attractions like Bedok Reservoir and East Coast Park. Bedok is well-connected by MRT stations on the East-West and Downtown Lines, and features dedicated cycling and pedestrian paths linking key recreational spaces. Retail and dining options are abundant, with Bedok Mall serving as a central hub connected to the Bedok Integrated Transport Hub, which houses both the MRT station and bus interchange.

As part of Singapore’s East Region, the area around Bagnall Haus benefits from a blend of residential, industrial, and commercial activity. The region is an aviation hub, home to Paya Lebar Air Base, Changi Naval Base, and several major airline headquarters. It also has strong manufacturing and business activity, including Changi Business Park, which hosts multinational corporations. Residents have access to numerous green spaces and parks, with future infrastructure enhancements like the Cross Island Line and Thomson-East Coast Line further improving connectivity.

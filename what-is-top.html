What is TOP and Why It Matters — A Buyer's Guide

By Jet Lee · 12 June 2026 · 4 min read

If you are buying a new launch condo in Singapore, you will hear the term TOP mentioned frequently. But what does it actually mean, and why should it matter to you?

TOP stands for Temporary Occupation Permit. It is the certification issued by the Building and Construction Authority (BCA) that allows you to legally occupy the unit you bought. Without TOP, you cannot move in.

What is TOP?

In simple terms, TOP is the official green light that says a building is safe and ready for occupation. It is issued after the developer completes construction and passes all required inspections — structural, fire safety, sanitary, and barrier-free accessibility.

Once the developer obtains TOP, you can:

  • Collect the keys to your unit
  • Move in or start renovations
  • Rent out the unit to tenants

TOP vs CSC: What's the Difference?

You may also hear about CSC (Certificate of Statutory Completion) or CQC (Certificate of Qualified Completion). Here is how they differ:

Document What it Means When
TOPYou can move in and occupy~3-5 years after launch
CSCAll statutory requirements fulfilled~6-12 months after TOP
CQCSimilar to CSC for certain projectsAfter TOP

Key point: You can move in immediately after TOP. CSC/CQC come later but do not affect your ability to occupy the unit.

How TOP Affects Your Payments

If you bought a new launch under the progressive payment scheme, TOP is a major milestone for your finances:

  • Before TOP: You pay in stages aligned with construction progress (e.g., foundation, framework, roof, etc.)
  • Upon TOP: You typically pay the final ~15-25% balance (including any outstanding loan amount)
  • After TOP: Your monthly mortgage instalments begin in full (before TOP, you only pay interest on the drawn-down amount)

This means your monthly cash flow changes significantly after TOP. It is important to plan ahead.

Typical TOP Timeline

Most new launch condos in Singapore have a TOP timeline of about 3 to 5 years from the launch date. Here is a rough guide:

  • Yr 1: Demolition (if applicable), foundation works, basement excavation
  • Yr 2: Structural framework, concrete pouring, block work
  • Yr 3: Roofing, M&E (mechanical & electrical), internal fitting
  • Yr 4: Finishing, testing, inspections, landscaping
  • TOP: Issued after all inspections pass

For example, ELTA at Clementi was launched in February 2025 and is expected to receive TOP in 2029 — approximately a 4-year construction timeline.

What Happens If TOP Is Delayed?

While most developers complete on time, delays can happen due to:

  • Supply chain issues (materials)
  • Labour shortages
  • Unforeseen site conditions
  • Regulatory changes

The Sale & Purchase Agreement usually includes a liquidated damages clause — meaning the developer must compensate you if TOP is delayed beyond the agreed date. Typically this is calculated at 10% per annum of the purchase price for the delay period.

Why TOP Matters to You

  • For owner-occupiers: TOP determines when you can move into your new home. Plan your current lease or HDB resale timeline accordingly
  • For investors: TOP is when you can start collecting rental income. Delays mean lost rental opportunity
  • For upgraders: If you plan to sell your current HDB, time it so you can move out close to your new condo's TOP date
  • For renovation: You can only start renovation works after TOP is issued

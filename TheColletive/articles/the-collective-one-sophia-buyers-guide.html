The Collective at One Sophia: A Buyer's Guide to D9's Newest Mixed Development

Published 15 August 2026 · New Launch Guide · Developer: Singhaiyi

The Collective at One Sophia is one of the most talked-about launches of 2026 — and for good reason. Rising on the site of the former Peace Centre at the crossroads of Selegie Road, Middle Road and Sophia Road, it brings 367 bespoke residences to one of Singapore's most storied addresses, District 9.

The quick facts: 367 residences · Studio to 3BR Luxury · 2 towers of 19 storeys · 99-year tenure from 6 Dec 2023 · TOP Dec 2029 · 4 MRT stations within 700m · Prices from $1.25M.

From Peace Centre to a New Landmark

For decades, Peace Centre was part of Singapore's furniture-shopping landscape. Its redevelopment into a mixed-use landmark — with 367 residences above 122 offices and 127 retail units — is a textbook example of how older commercial sites in the city centre are being transformed for a new generation of living.

The residential towers sit above a 13-storey office and retail podium, with a spectacular suspended garden designed by French architect Jean Francois Milou of studioMilou, working with ADDP Architects. The result is a genuine live-work-play address: premium offices and a curated retail mall at your doorstep, and one of Singapore's best-connected locations around you.

Location: Four MRT Stations Within 700m

Few new launches can match The Collective's connectivity. Four MRT stations sit within a short walk:

  • Bencoolen MRT — approximately 450m (Downtown Line)
  • Rochor MRT — approximately 500m (Downtown Line)
  • Dhoby Ghaut MRT — approximately 600m (North-South, North-East & Circle Lines — Singapore's largest interchange)
  • Bras Basah MRT — approximately 700m (Circle Line)

That means you can reach the CBD, Marina Bay, Orchard Road or the airport without ever needing a car. For investors and owner-occupiers alike, this level of accessibility is a durable advantage.

Whether you're a young professional looking for a smart city pied-à-terre, a family upgrading to a 3BR, or an investor seeking a well-located asset in the core central region, the mix covers you.

The Singhaiyi Story

The developer, Sophia Residential Pte. Ltd., is part of the Singhaiyi group — a developer with a growing track record of city-fringe and city-centre projects. The architecture, by ADDP Architects with studioMilou's Jean Francois Milou, is the signature element here: Milou is known for museum-grade design, and his suspended garden concept for The Collective is a talking point in itself.

Facilities: Designed Around Social Living

The development's facilities lean into the "collective" idea — spaces to gather, work out and unwind:

  • Azure Pool & Azure Spa — resort-style water facilities
  • Gymnasium & Fitness Zone
  • The SOcial Club and Games Room
  • Lounge Deck, The Patio & The Playpen (kids' play area)
  • Sky Alcove on levels 5, 7, 9, 12, 14 and 19
  • One SOcial — outdoor lounge at Level 19 with city views

Who Is This For?

The Collective at One Sophia suits three buyer profiles especially well:

  • Young professionals who want to live close to work, Orchard Road and the arts enclave — and who value MRT connectivity above all.
  • Investors looking for a District 9 asset with strong rental appeal (the area's office and retail podium adds to the address's liveability).
  • Empty nesters who want to downsize into a smaller, well-located home without leaving the city they love.

My Take — 18 Years in the Market

Location is the moat. A 99-year leasehold in the core central region with four MRT stations within 700m and a mixed-use anchor at its base is a rare combination at this price point. The Peace Centre site was undervalued for years; Singhaiyi's transformation of it is one of the more interesting urban-renewal stories of the current cycle. For buyers who value convenience and culture, this is a project worth serious consideration — especially with TOP not expected until Dec 2029, which spreads out the payment timeline.

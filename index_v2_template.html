Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ELTA Clementi

Where is ELTA located?
ELTA is at 10 & 12 Clementi Avenue 1, District 5, Singapore. About 1km from Clementi MRT, near NUS, Nan Hua Primary, AYE and PIE.
What unit types does ELTA offer?
ELTA offers 501 units from 1-Bedroom + Study (506 sqft) to 5-Bedroom (1,776 sqft), including 3BR, 4BR, 4BR Premium, 4BR Dual Key, and 4BR+Study configurations.
How many units are still available?
Approximately 90 balance units remain across all types. Larger layouts like 4BR and 5BR have the most availability.
When is the expected TOP for ELTA?
ELTA's expected TOP is 2029. Construction is underway on both 39-storey towers.
Who is the developer of ELTA?
ELTA is developed by MCL Land and CSC Land Group. MCL Land is known for Parc Esta, J Gateway, Lake Grande. CSC Land is the property arm of CSC Holdings.
What schools are near ELTA?
Within 1km of Nan Hua Primary School (top school). Also near NUS High School, The Japanese School, NUS, and Singapore Polytechnic.
How far is ELTA from Clementi MRT?
Approximately 1km, about 10-12 min walk. Nearby Clementi bus interchange and AYE/PIE access.
What facilities does ELTA have?
Two 39-storey towers with pool, gym, function rooms, children's playground, BBQ areas and lush landscaping.
What shopping is near ELTA?
Clementi Mall (next to MRT), 321 Clementi, and Grantral Mall with supermarkets, food courts and clinics.
How do I register interest?

People Also Ask

What Is a Good Class Bungalow (GCB)?

Good Class Bungalows are Singapore's most exclusive landed housing category. Governed by the Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA), GCBs must meet strict criteria:

  • Minimum land area: 1,400 square metres (15,070 sq ft)
  • Maximum height: 2 storeys
  • Location: Only within designated GCB Areas — there are just 39 such areas across Singapore
  • Approximately 2,800 GCBs exist in the whole of Singapore — making them rarer than many people realise

Unlike private condominiums, GCBs are not subject to foreign ownership restrictions — any nationality can purchase a GCB (subject to approval), which adds an international dimension to the neighbourhood's character.

The Old Holland & Brizay Park GCB Enclave

Amberwood at Holland sits on Holland Link, right at the edge of the Old Holland GCB area and minutes from the Brizay Park GCB enclave. This is one of Singapore's most established and sought-after landed residential neighbourhoods.

The area is characterised by:

  • Generous land plots — many GCBs sit on land parcels well above the minimum 1,400 sqm requirement
  • Mature trees and greenery — the neighbourhood is lush, quiet, and feels a world away from the city
  • Low traffic — cul-de-sacs and quiet lanes define the streetscape
  • Privacy — bungalows are set back from the road behind gates and greenery

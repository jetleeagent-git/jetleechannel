Thomson Reserve Preview & Launch Guide 2026

Updated June 2026 · Expected Preview: October 2026

Thomson Reserve (汤申保留地) is one of the most anticipated new launch condos in Singapore for 2026. With an expected preview in October 2026, this mega development by UOL Group, CapitaLand Development, and Singapore Land Group has already generated significant interest among homebuyers and investors.

This guide covers everything you need to know about the launch — from the timeline and how to register, to what to expect at the showflat and the booking process.

Launch Timeline (Current Status)

As of June 2026, the following timeline applies. Please note all dates are subject to authorities' approval and may change:

  • Targeted Preview: October 2026 — Invitation-only preview for registered VVIPs and early interest parties. E-brochure and indicative price guide typically released around this time.
  • Public Launch: After preview phase — Following the preview period, units are opened to the general public.
  • Balloting: As needed — If demand exceeds supply, a balloting exercise will be conducted by the developer, usually on the launch day.
  • Estimated TOP: TBC — To be confirmed. Major projects of this scale typically take 3–5 years from preview.
Important: The preview date of October 2026 is a targeted timeline. Official confirmation will come from the developers closer to the date. Register your interest early to receive updates directly.

How to Register for the VVIP Preview

The preview phase is typically by appointment and prioritises those who have registered interest early. Here's what to do:

  1. Register your interest on the official Thomson Reserve website — thomson-reserve-direct-developer.com — via the contact form.
  2. Meet or speak with the developer's appointed sales team to indicate your unit preferences, budget range, and any specific requirements.
  3. Submit your cheque (if required) during the preview booking phase to secure your queue number.
  4. Attend the showflat on your allocated date/time slot during the preview period.

What to Expect at the Showflat

For a project of this scale (approximately 1,268 units), the showflat is expected to be comprehensive. Typical elements include:

  • Show units — Fully furnished examples of selected unit types (usually 1+S, 2BR, 3BR, 4BR)
  • Site model — A scale model showing the master plan, block positions, landscaping, and facilities layout
  • Material board — Samples of finishes, flooring, countertops, sanitary ware, and fittings
  • Price list — Unit-by-unit pricing (released during preview)
  • QQ (Quantum & Quantity) sheets — Charts showing available units across price points

Types of Buyers Attending

  • Owner-occupiers — Families drawn by Ai Tong School proximity, nature setting, and spacious floor plans
  • Upgraders — Existing HDB flat owners in Bishan, Ang Mo Kio, and Upper Thomson looking to move to private property while staying in the same neighbourhood
  • Investors — Attracted by the three blue-chip developers' track record, Mega development status, and rental demand from families and nature seekers
  • Foreign buyers — Those eligible to purchase, attracted by the unique reservoir-side location

Frequently Asked Questions

When exactly will the Thomson Reserve preview start?

The targeted preview is October 2026, subject to authorities' approval. The exact date will be confirmed closer to the launch. Early registrants will receive SMS and email notifications.

How do I book an appointment to view the showflat?

Register your interest on the official website. The sales team will contact you to arrange an appointment. Walk-ins may be accepted during the public launch phase but appointments are preferred.

Will there be a balloting exercise?

Very likely, given the high level of interest. Developers typically conduct balloting on launch day when demand outstrips supply. Early registration gives you a better queue position.

Where will the showflat be located?

The showflat location for Thomson Reserve has not been confirmed yet. It will likely be set up near the actual site at Bright Hill Drive or at a central location in Singapore. Registered buyers will be informed.

Can I see floor plans before the preview?

Floor plans are typically released during the preview phase. Some indicative layouts may be shared with serious buyers beforehand. Contact the sales team for the latest information.

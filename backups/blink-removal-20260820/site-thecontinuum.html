A Premium Freehold Address

The Continuum is a freehold condominium launch by Hoi Hup Realty & Sunway, located at 1-8 Thiam Siew Avenue in District 15. Set along Thiam Siew Avenue (Tanjong Katong), this new address brings together scale, permanence, and a strong residential identity.

Designed by P&T Consultants across a substantial plot size of 269,995 sqft, The Continuum presents a rare freehold offering of notable presence. With Dakota MRT Station within easy reach and the vibrant Paya Lebar hub minutes away, this is where convenience meets the enduring charm of Tanjong Katong.

Everything Within Reach

The Continuum sits in the heart of District 15's most beloved residential enclave — Tanjong Katong. Dakota MRT Station is within walking distance, providing seamless connectivity across Singapore via the Circle Line.

The Paya Lebar regional hub is minutes away with malls like PLQ Mall, KINEX, Paya Lebar Square, and Singapore Post Centre — offering supermarkets, dining, retail, and entertainment. To the east, enjoy the iconic Katong dining scene with its famous Peranakan cuisine, trendy cafés, and lifestyle offerings.

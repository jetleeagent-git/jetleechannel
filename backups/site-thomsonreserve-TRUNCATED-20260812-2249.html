Project Overview

A Storied Site,
Reimagined

Welcome to Thomson Reserve (汤申保留地), the landmark redevelopment of the former Thomson View Condominium — a beloved Upper Thomson icon reborn as a magnificent mega development nestled beside MacRitchie Reservoir.

Jointly developed by UOL Group, CapitaLand Development and Singapore Land Group, Thomson Reserve brings together three of Singapore's most esteemed developers. From 1+Study to 5-Bedroom homes, every unit is designed with over 80% of the site dedicated to lush landscaping — a true sanctuary in the city.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You
Need to Know

What is Thomson Reserve?+
Thomson Reserve is the official name for the redevelopment of the former Thomson View Condominium at Bright Hill Drive, District 20. The name was confirmed in January 2026, replacing the earlier working name "Thomson View Residences". The site was sold en bloc for S$810 million in 2024 and acquired on 2 October 2025.
Who are the developers?+
Thomson Reserve is jointly developed by UOL Group, CapitaLand Development, and Singapore Land Group (SingLand) — three of Singapore's most reputable property developers with extensive track records in delivering quality residential and mixed-use developments.
When is the preview and launch?+
Thomson Reserve is targeted to preview in October 2026. The exact launch date will be communicated to registered guests ahead of the official showflat opening. Register your interest to be among the first to receive updates and VVIP preview invitations.
How many units and what types are available?+
Thomson Reserve will comprise approximately 1,268 residential units (TBC). The development sits on a sprawling 504,314 sqft site with Plot Ratio 2.1.
What is the tenure?+
Thomson Reserve is a 99-year leasehold development, with the lease commencing from October 2025.
Which MRT stations serve Thomson Reserve?+
Thomson Reserve is right next to Upper Thomson MRT Station and directly opposite Thomson Plaza. Bright Hill MRT and Mayflower MRT are also nearby — all on the Thomson–East Coast Line (TEL).
When is the estimated TOP?+
The estimated TOP date is To Be Confirmed (TBC), subject to authorities' approval.
What is the land cost advantage of Thomson Reserve?+
Thomson Reserve was acquired at $1,178 psf/ppr — an RCR (Rest of Central Region) site priced below OCR (Outside Central Region) land costs. For comparison, OCR sites like Chuan Grove ($1,331–$1,376 psf/ppr) and Bedok Rise ($1,330 psf/ppr) were acquired at higher land rates, making Thomson Reserve an exceptionally good value proposition.
What is Thomson Plaza and how close is it?+
Thomson Plaza is a popular neighbourhood mall with a wide variety of dining, retail and lifestyle options. Thomson Reserve is directly opposite Thomson Plaza — residents can walk there in minutes. The Upper Thomson enclave is also well known for its vibrant food street with local and international eateries.
What facilities will Thomson Reserve offer?+
Thomson Reserve's draft site plan includes a Tennis Court, Multi-Purpose Court, Clubhouse, BBQ Pavilion, Swimming Pool, Kids Pool / Playzone, Gym, Pavilion, Playground, and Fitness Corner — subject to authorities' approval.

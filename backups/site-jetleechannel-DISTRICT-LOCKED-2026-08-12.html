Frequently Asked Questions

Singapore real estate — answered by an insider with 17+ years of experience.

It depends on your budget, timeline, and goals. HDB resale prices are moderating but COV has risen to $15K–$35K. Condo supply is tightening. If you have $600K–$1.2M, a resale HDB in a mature estate offers value. At $1.5M+, a suburban condo near MRT gives better appreciation. WhatsApp Jet Lee for a personalised comparison.
Cash Over Valuation (COV) is the cash amount paid above HDB valuation. It has jumped from $5K–$15K in 2025 to $15K–$35K in 2026 due to fewer BTO flats and cash-rich upgraders. Get a valuation report before house hunting.
For HDB flats the lease must cover the youngest buyer to age 95. For condos: minimum 20 years remaining lease. You can use up to 100% of OA savings for downpayment and monthly instalments. Ask Jet Lee for a CPF calculation.
Browse the Projects section for the latest launches, balance units, and previews. Or WhatsApp Jet Lee directly.
As of mid-2026, rates are at 2.8%–3.2%, expected to ease to ~2.5% by end 2026. Fixed-rate packages remain most popular. Contact Jet Lee for a free loan comparison.
Seller's Stamp Duty applies when selling within the first 3 years of purchase: 12% (year 1), 8% (year 2), 4% (year 3). After 3 years, SSD is zero. Plan to hold for at least 4 years.
BTO is cheaper ($300K–$500K for 4-room) but takes 4–5 years. Resale costs more ($500K–$700K+) but you can move in immediately. With ~8,500 BTO flats/year, supply is tight. Apply for BTO in non-mature estates while watching resale prices.

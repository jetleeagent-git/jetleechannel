Project Overview

A Garden Home. A Home Garden.

Discover the beautiful connection with nature at The Arcady at Boon Keng — an urban oasis garden where every single day is filled with the joys of nature.

The Arcady at Boon Keng is a nature paradise by the busy city fringe. An architectural masterpiece harmoniously integrated with wonderful botanicals and a full range of family-oriented indulgences, inspired by mountainside living.

This upcoming 172-unit freehold condominium at 2 Saint Barnabas Lane (Serangoon Road) offers a rare sense of permanence in District 12. With a single 24-storey block, the development introduces a well-defined private residential experience that feels exclusive yet substantial. Designed by Park + Associates, every detail points to a home that is modern, refined, and carefully considered for today's homeowners.

The Arcady offers a broad selection of layouts including 1BR + Study, 2BR, 2BR + Study, 3BR, 3BR Premium, 3BR Premium + Study, 4BR + Study, 4BR Premium, and Penthouse residences.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Arcady at Boon Keng? ▼
The Arcady at Boon Keng is a brand new freehold condominium at 2 Saint Barnabas Lane, District 12. It comprises 172 units within a single 24-storey block, ranging from 1-bedroom + study to 4-bedroom premium and penthouses, developed by KSH Holdings, SLB Development and Ho Lee Group.
What is the tenure of The Arcady? ▼
The Arcady at Boon Keng is Freehold (Estate in Perpetuity), offering the enduring value and legacy of permanent land ownership.
Which MRT stations are nearby? ▼
Boon Keng MRT (North East Line) is approximately 440m away (~6 min walk). Geylang Bahru MRT (Downtown Line) is approximately 870m away (~10 min walk). Major expressways including PIE, CTE and KPE are within a 2-minute drive.
What unit types are available? ▼
The Arcady offers 1BR + Study (527 sqft), 2BR (667–678 sqft), 2BR + Study (764 sqft), 3BR (969–980 sqft), 3BR Premium (1,087–1,195 sqft), 3BR Premium + Study (1,281–1,486 sqft), 4BR + Study (1,410 sqft), 4BR Premium (1,625 sqft), and Penthouse (2,433–2,583 sqft).
Who is the developer? ▼
The Arcady at Boon Keng is developed by KSH Ultra Unity Pte. Ltd., a joint venture between KSH Holdings Limited, SLB Development Ltd., and Ho Lee Group — three trusted home-grown Singapore developers with decades of combined experience.
When is the expected TOP? ▼
The expected date of vacant possession is 31 December 2028, with expected legal completion by 31 December 2031 or 3 years after notice of vacant possession, whichever is earlier.

ELTA Clementi — Developer Review

By Jet Lee · 12 June 2026 · 6 min read

ELTA is one of the most talked-about new launches in Clementi in recent years. Developed by a joint venture between MCL Land and CSC Land Group, this 501-unit project made headlines with a strong 65% take-up on launch weekend in February 2025.

In this review, I break down everything you need to know about ELTA — the developers, the project specs, unit types, location, and its investment potential.

About the Developers

MCL Land

MCL Land is a well-established Singapore developer with a portfolio of quality residential projects. They are known for developments such as Parc Esta, J Gateway, Lake Grande, and Riversails. MCL Land has a reputation for solid construction quality, thoughtful layouts, and reliable after-sales service. Their projects tend to age well and maintain good resale value.

CSC Land Group

CSC Land Group is the property development arm of CSC Holdings, a Singapore-listed company. They have delivered projects including Parc Life and The Santorini. Their collaboration with MCL Land on ELTA brings together MCL's residential expertise and CSC's development capability.

Track record note: MCL Land has a strong track record with former en-bloc sites like Parc Esta (near Eunos MRT) which saw good capital appreciation. The MCL-CSC partnership on ELTA is their second joint project, signalling confidence in the Clementi location.

Location Analysis

ELTA sits on Clementi Avenue 1, about 1km from Clementi MRT and bus interchange. Key surrounding amenities:

  • MRT: Clementi (East-West Line) — ~10 min walk
  • Schools: Nan Hua Primary (within 1km), NUS High School, The Japanese School, NUS, Singapore Polytechnic
  • Malls: Clementi Mall, 321 Clementi
  • Expressways: AYE and PIE
  • Parks: Clementi Woods, West Coast Park
  • Future: One-north and Jurong Lake District developments

Investment Potential

Several factors support ELTA's investment outlook:

  • Limited supply in Clementi — ELTA is the only new launch in Clementi. The last major launch was Clavon (2020) and Parc Clematis (2019). With limited new supply, existing and upcoming projects benefit from pent-up demand
  • NUS and one-north rental demand — The knowledge corridor continues to grow, attracting professionals, researchers, and academics who prefer to rent within a short commute
  • Price appreciation — Launch price averaged ~$2,537 psf. For context, nearby Clavon resale units are now trading at $1,800-$2,100 psf. The differential reflects ELTA's newer product and longer lease
  • Smart home features — All units come with smart home integration, a differentiating factor that appeals to tech-savvy buyers and tenants

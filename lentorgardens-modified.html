Lentor Gardens Residences is a new launch condominium in District 26, Singapore, developed by Kingsford Lentor Development Pte Ltd. It comprises 499 residential units across 3 blocks of 16-storey, 1 block of 8-storey, and 3 exclusive strata terrace houses — with unit types from 2-bedroom to 4-bedroom.
Located at 66, 74, 76, 78 Lentor Gardens, Singapore 788840 in District 26. It sits beside the future Hillock Park (NParks) and is a short walk from Lentor MRT (TE5) on the Thomson-East Coast Line.
Approximately 3 minutes walk from Lentor MRT station (TE5) on the Thomson-East Coast Line — providing direct access to Orchard Road, CBD, Marina Bay, and eventually Changi Airport.

Strata Terrace (5BR) — 1,496 sqft (3 exclusive units)
Developed by Kingsford Lentor Development Pte Ltd, part of the award-winning Kingsford Group. Their Singapore portfolio includes Normanton Park (1,862 units), Chuan Park (916 units), The Hill @ One-North (142 units), and One Marina Gardens (937 units).
Lentor Gardens Residences is a 99-year leasehold development, with the lease commencing from 7 July 2025. Estimated TOP (Temporary Occupation Permit) is expected around 2029.
Over 43 facilities including: 75m Skyline Pool, 50m Lap Pool, Hydro Jet Pool, Serenity Lagoon, Aqua Therapy Bed, Sky Terraces, BBQ Pavilions, Splash Cove, Wading Lagoon, Gym, Yoga Studio, Clubhouse (3 function rooms), Tennis Court, Sky View Deck, Infinity Pool Deck, and Early Childhood Development Centre.
Anderson Primary School (within 1km), CHIJ St Nicholas Girls' School, Mayflower Primary School, Presbyterian High School, Yio Chu Kang Secondary School, and Anderson Serangoon Junior College.

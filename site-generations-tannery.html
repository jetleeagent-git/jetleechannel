Why Freehold Industrial Matters

Freehold industrial is one of the scarcest asset classes in Singapore. Almost every new industrial launch in the pipeline is leasehold — typically 30 or 60-year JTC tenure. Generations @ Tannery breaks that pattern.

  • ✓ No lease decay — value doesn't erode as the lease runs down
  • ✓ Full bank loan tenure — easier financing vs shorter-lease industrial
  • ✓ Intergenerational wealth — pass it down without lease expiry pressure
  • ✓ 0% ABSD for all buyers — no tax penalty regardless of citizenship or property count
  • ✓ Long-term upside from Paya Lebar Air Base redevelopment and Kallang Alive Masterplan

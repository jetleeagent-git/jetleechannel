Project Overview

A New Chapter Begins at Turf City

Welcome to Dunearn House (达恩·豪庭), the first private residential development at the landmark new Bukit Timah Turf City — a reimagined estate that recalls its century-long equestrian heritage while embracing a vibrant future.

Positioned along Dunearn Road, within minutes' walk of Sixth Avenue MRT, Dunearn House offers seamless connectivity alongside lush greenery, the Bukit Timah-Rochor Green Corridor, and a world-class community being built around you.

Developed by a distinguished joint venture of Frasers Property, CSC Land and Sekisui House, this is an address designed for generations.

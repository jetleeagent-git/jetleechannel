Lucerne Grand Review — CDL's Lakeside New Launch Worth Watching

Published July 15, 2026 · by Jet Lee

Every few years, a new launch comes along that combines location, developer pedigree, and macro tailwinds in a way that's hard to ignore. Lucerne Grand — City Developments Limited's (CDL) upcoming project at Lakeside Drive — is shaping up to be exactly that.

With Lakeside MRT at its doorstep, Jurong Lake Gardens as its backyard, and the Jurong Lake District (JLD) transformation accelerating, this project sits right at the intersection of convenience and long-term growth. Let's break down what makes it worth watching.

Location, Location, Location

MRT Doorstep — Literally

Lucerne Grand is built right at the doorstep of Lakeside MRT (EW26) on the East-West Line. That means you're:

  • 2 stops to Jurong East (JEM, Westgate, IMM)
  • 12 minutes to Raffles Place (direct train)
  • 15 minutes to City Hall

For drivers, the PIE and AYE are both minutes away, giving you direct access to the CBD and Changi Airport in under 20 and 25 minutes respectively (off-peak).

Jurong Lake Gardens — Your Backyard

Singapore's third national garden is literally next door. At 90 hectares, Jurong Lake Gardens offers waterfront promenades, cycling paths, the newly revamped Chinese and Japanese Gardens, and community spaces that rival Gardens by the Bay in scale if not in tourist footfall. For residents, this is daily lifestyle gold — not weekend excursion territory.

Schools Within 1KM

Families take note — Rulang Primary School, Shuqun Primary, Lakeside Primary, and Boon Lay Garden Primary are all within 1km. Rulang in particular is a well-regarded school that adds to the family appeal of this location.

The JLD Transformation — Why This Matters

This is arguably the strongest macro story behind Lucerne Grand. The Jurong Lake District (JLD) is Singapore's second CBD — a government-mandated transformation zone that will bring:

  • 100,000+ new jobs in the JLD business district
  • 20,000 new homes in a master-planned lakeside precinct
  • The Jurong Region Line (JRL) — opening from 2027, which will supercharge connectivity
  • Major commercial hubs including the JLD Gateway mixed-use developments

When the government decides to build a second CBD, property values in the surrounding areas tend to follow. Jurong East has already seen significant price appreciation — Lakeside, as the next station down, is positioned to benefit from the spillover.

CDL — A Developer You Can Trust

City Developments Limited (CDL) is one of Singapore's most established developers. They've delivered landmark projects like Marina One Residences, The Tapestry, and Whistler Grand. Their track record for quality, design, and timely delivery is well-documented. For buyers, this de-risks the purchase considerably — CDL doesn't cut corners.

Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • Lakeside MRT doorstep — exceptional connectivity
  • Jurong Lake Gardens next door
  • JLD transformation = strong growth story
  • CDL — established, trusted developer
  • Good school network within 1km
  • Diverse unit mix (2BR to 4BR Premium)

⚠️ Watch Out For

  • Jurong area — premium not central
  • 99-year leasehold (standard for new launches)
  • Pricing yet to be announced — could be steep given land costs
  • Completion timeline — likely 2029-2030

Who Should Consider Lucerne Grand?

HDB upgraders from the West — If you're living in Jurong, Boon Lay, or nearby estates and looking to upgrade to a condo without leaving your familiar area, this is going to be a natural choice.

Investors betting on JLD — The second CBD narrative is real, and Lakeside is the next station from Jurong East that still has launch-day pricing. Early-bird buyers often capture the most upside.

Families who want space + nature — With Jurong Lake Gardens as your backyard and multiple good schools nearby, this could be a strong family proposition.

Bottom Line

Lucerne Grand has the ingredients of a compelling launch: prime location beside an MRT and a national garden, a top-tier developer, and a macro growth story in JLD that's government-backed. The key variable will be pricing — if CDL prices it competitively given the land cost, this could be one of the more attractive OCR launches of 2026.

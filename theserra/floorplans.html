Development Site Layout & Building Setbacks

The Serra Residences features a single 28-storey luxury residential tower situated in a quiet residential enclave along Bassein Road. The site design prioritizes tranquility with generous building setbacks (approx. 16m to 27m), ground floor landscape bay, arrival driveway, multi-level carpark, sky terrace on Level 17, and swimming pool facilities.

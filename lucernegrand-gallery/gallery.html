About Lucerne Grand

Lucerne Grand is a 99-year leasehold condominium development by City Developments Limited (CDL), located at Lakeside Drive, District 22 (Jurong) — with Lakeside MRT (EW26) at its doorstep.

The development offers 2-bedroom to 4-bedroom premium units (approx. 620 – 1,430 sq ft), designed around a resort-style living experience within Singapore's emerging second Central Business District — the Jurong Lake District (JLD).

Target preview: Mid September 2026. Official marketing agent: PropNex Realty.

Location Highlights

  • Lakeside MRT (EW26) — steps away, direct East-West Line connectivity
  • Jurong Lake Gardens — Singapore's third national garden at your doorstep
  • JEM, Westgate, IMM & Boon Lay Shopping Centre within minutes
  • PIE and AYE expressways for seamless islandwide travel
  • Within 1km: Rulang Primary, Shuqun Primary, Lakeside Primary, Boon Lay Garden Primary

A Development By CDL

City Developments Limited is one of Singapore's largest and most established property developers, with a track record of award-winning residential, commercial and hospitality projects across the globe.

Narra Residences is a new 99-year leasehold condominium nestled along Dairy Farm Walk in District 23. Developed by a consortium led by Santarli Realty and Apex Asia Development, this 540-unit sanctuary offers a rare blend of natural tranquillity and urban connectivity.
The development features 3 blocks of 6 storeys, 2 blocks of 13 storeys, and 2 blocks of 16 storeys — set within lushly landscaped grounds with resort-style facilities. With Hillview MRT just minutes away and the Bukit Timah Nature Reserve at your doorstep, Narra Residences redefines hillside living in Singapore.

Narra Residences is developed by a joint venture consortium led by Santarli Realty and Apex Asia Development, alongside Soon Li Heng Civil Engineering and Kay Lim Realty. This partnership brings together deep expertise in residential development, construction excellence, and a shared commitment to quality.

With P&T Consultants as architect, Kimly Construction as main contractor, and Ecoplan Asia handling landscape design, Narra Residences is built to the highest standards of design, sustainability, and liveability.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question to expand.

What is the tenure of Narra Residences?+
99-year leasehold commencing from 20 January 2025.
How many units does Narra Residences have?+
540 residential units across 7 blocks, plus 4 commercial shops at ground level.
When is the expected TOP?+
Expected TOP is 20 January 2030. Legal completion by 20 January 2033.
What unit types are available?+
2 Bedroom, 3 Bedroom, 4 Bedroom, 5 Bedroom, and Penthouse layouts.
Who is the developer?+
Dairy Farm Walk JV Development Pte Ltd — a consortium led by Santarli Realty and Apex Asia Development, together with Soon Li Heng and Kay Lim Realty.
Where is Narra Residences located?+
50–64 Dairy Farm Walk, within District 23 (Bukit Panjang / Hillview).

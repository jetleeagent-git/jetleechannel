Clementi MRT Condos — A Buyer's Guide

By Jet Lee · 12 June 2026 · 5 min read

Clementi MRT Station (EW23) on the East-West Line is one of the most well-connected stations in Singapore's city fringe. With direct train access to the CBD in under 25 minutes, a major bus interchange, and a full range of amenities at Clementi Mall, it is no surprise that condos near Clementi MRT are always in demand.

In this guide, I cover the best condominiums within walking distance of Clementi MRT, their distances, and what makes each one worth considering.

Why Live Near Clementi MRT?

  • Quick commute: 5 stops to Raffles Place (CBD), 2 stops to Jurong East, 1 stop to Dover (for Singapore Polytechnic)
  • Bus interchange: Serves western Singapore with extensive bus connections
  • Clementi Mall: Directly above the MRT, with supermarkets, retail, and F&B
  • Schools: Nan Hua Primary, NUS High, The Japanese School, NUS, SP
  • Future growth: Jurong Lake District and one-north continue to drive value

Condos Near Clementi MRT — Ranked by Distance

1. ELTA (Upcoming, ~1km to MRT)

Location: Clementi Avenue 1
Walk to MRT: ~10-12 minutes
Tenure: 99-year leasehold
Units: 501

The newest launch in Clementi. ELTA offers a modern living experience with smart home features, dual towers at 39 storeys, and a full suite of facilities. It is a short walk to Clementi MRT via the covered walkway along Clementi Avenue 1, passing by Nan Hua Primary School.

Best for: Buyers who want a brand new condo with the latest specifications and the longest lease in Clementi's current new launch market.

Visit eltasingapore.jetleechannel.sg for floor plans, site plan, and balance unit prices.

2. Clavon (~900m to MRT)

Location: Clementi Avenue 1
Walk to MRT: ~9-11 minutes
Completed: 2023
Units: 640

Clavon is a quality UOL development sitting right next to ELTA. Completed in 2023, it is relatively new and offers a similar walk to the MRT. The development is popular among families due to Nan Hua Primary's proximity.

Best for: Those who want a relatively new completed condo without waiting for TOP. Good for immediate move-in.

3. Parc Clematis (~1.2km to MRT)

Location: Jalan Lempeng
Walk to MRT: ~13-15 minutes
Completed: 2024
Units: 1,468

A massive resort-style development with three themed zones. Parc Clematis offers 400,000 sqft of facilities — one of the largest in the area. While slightly further from the MRT than ELTA or Clavon, the sheer scale of facilities and the variety of unit types make it a strong contender.

Best for: Families who want resort-style amenities and don't mind a slightly longer walk to the station.

4. The Trilinq (~1.4km to MRT)

Location: Jalan Lempeng
Walk to MRT: ~15-17 minutes
Completed: 2016
Units: 755

The Trilinq has mature landscaping and a more established feel. Its three distinctive towers create a landmark presence in the Clementi skyline. A bus service from outside the condo connects to Clementi MRT in about 5 minutes.

Best for: Resale buyers who value mature greenery and proven rental demand.

5. Clementiwoods (~800m to MRT)

Location: Clementi Avenue 4
Walk to MRT: ~8-10 minutes
Completed: 2014
Units: 241

The closest to Clementi MRT on this list. Clementiwoods is a boutique development with only 241 units, offering a more intimate living experience. Its location on the quieter Clementi Avenue 4 means less traffic noise.

Best for: Those who prioritise MRT proximity and prefer a smaller, quieter community.

Rental Potential Near Clementi MRT

Clementi MRT condos consistently enjoy strong rental demand thanks to:

  • NUS and SP students — A steady stream of tenants looking for quality housing near campus
  • One-north professionals — Researchers, engineers, and tech workers prefer the short commute
  • Families — Proximity to Nan Hua Primary and other top schools attracts renting families
  • CBD commuters — The direct MRT line to Raffles Place makes it popular with professionals

Typical rental yields for condos in this area range from 3% to 4%, depending on the project and unit type. Smaller units (1BR, 2BR) tend to achieve higher per-sqft rental rates.

Future Developments

Clementi and the surrounding area continue to evolve:

  • Jurong Lake District — Singapore's second CBD, with new office and commercial space
  • One-north expansion — Ongoing development of the innovation hub
  • Clementi NorthArc — New BTO and community facilities near the area

These developments will continue to support property values and rental demand for condos near Clementi MRT.

Which One Should You Choose?

  • For brand new with longest lease: ELTA — the freshest product with progressive payment
  • For immediate move-in near MRT: Clementiwoods or Clavon
  • For resort facilities: Parc Clematis
  • For established resale with greenery: The Trilinq

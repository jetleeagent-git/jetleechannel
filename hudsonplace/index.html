Project Overview

A New Chapter
in Queenstown

Hudson Place Residences is a landmark development at Media Circle within the historic Queenstown planning district — now transformed as part of the One-North innovation corridor.

Two elegantly elevated towers of 23 and 15 storeys rise above a lushly landscaped podium. Unobstructed views sweep across Wessex Estate's heritage greenery toward the Greater Southern Waterfront.

With vacant possession expected September 2029, Hudson Place delivers a rare convergence of city connectivity, heritage, nature, and forward-thinking design in Singapore's most dynamic growth precinct.

+
Hudson Place is located within the Media Circle precinct in District 5, close to one-north, Mediapolis, Science Park, and the future Queenstown Node transformation area.
Why do buyers believe Hudson Place has upside potential?
+
Many buyers are focusing on the long-term transformation of one-north and Media Circle. As more tech companies, AI firms, media businesses, and innovation hubs expand into the area, housing demand may continue growing over time.
Is Hudson Place good for investment?
+
Many investors are attracted to Hudson Place because of its proximity to one-north, Mediapolis, Science Park, NUH, and business parks. The project may appeal to future tenants working within the innovation and tech corridor.
Is Hudson Place good for own stay?
+
Yes. Some buyers prefer Hudson Place because of its quieter environment compared to more crowded city-fringe locations, while still being near future growth areas and employment hubs.
What unit types are available at Hudson Place?

Hudson Place offers a range of layouts from 2-Bedroom units to larger 3-Bedroom and 4-Bedroom family homes, catering to both investors and own-stay buyers.

Is Hudson Place considered expensive for District 5?
